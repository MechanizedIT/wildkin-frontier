# Rootbound showcase — practical asset and scenery review

## Latest owner direction

Chris wants one diverse, full, living habitat showcase that invites exploration, close looking, harvesting and observing Wildkin. Continue Rootbound and its constituent parts. Use attractive useful models even when they differ from the target; a target mismatch or numerical score alone is not a rejection. This supersedes older fixed visual-score thresholds for the focused Rootbound run. The target remains design guidance, and actual player-view appearance and use decide retention.

## What the reviewer should consider

Alongside the actual image/model review, briefly ask which parts can be reused: wood, root, foliage, fungi, moss, stone, or other meaningful components. Name promising existing pieces before requesting new ones. A whole-object weakness does not discard a good component. Proposed resource yields are design ideas until mapped to an implemented harvest contract; avoid inventing special drops or item IDs.

Consider a few deliberate arrangements, rotations, modest scale changes and related tints. Judge whether they create believable local variety rather than arbitrary scatter. Preserve grounded supports, plausible anatomy, material identity, attachment contact and correct transforms. Moss fitted to one surface needs reconforming on another; a plant cannot float after its supporting part is harvested. One parent may own composite damage with individually removable children, subject to the existing owner-proposed harvesting/regrowth design.

For organic structural pieces, consider a spline/curve-driven root or bent trunk when it would provide useful variants. A practical authoring option is a centerline with varying section width/depth, taper and twist, with a fixed attachment/base frame. Bake a few reviewed meshes first; a runtime deformation system is not implied. Changes must update attached children, ground support, mesh normals and any relevant collider together. Reuse an attractive fixed mesh when bending adds little.

## Decision and proof

The short verdict should say **use**, **use with specific debt**, **repair one consequential defect**, or **hold for a concrete blocker**, and identify reusable parts/variation opportunities. A score may summarize observations but does not veto an attractive useful result. Do not require another generated image solely because a good shape differs stylistically from the target; a deliberate material/shape adaptation can be recorded in its construction plan.

Separate visible benefit from correctness. Check actual in-game composition for admission and retain focused proofs for changed collision, attachment, animation, harvesting, persistence and loading. Do not turn every static prop into an exhaustive topology research exercise. An intentionally multipart object need not be a single connected mesh; unexpected debris, open visible holes and unsupported parts still need judgment. Substantive physical/state defects cannot be excused as artistic differences.

Prefer using a promising asset in one reviewable Rootbound arrangement, then refining it against actual play, over repeatedly polishing isolated studies. Keep bounded attempts, independent review, original masters, main-only cohesive commits, one heavy job, unrelated-work preservation and no push.
