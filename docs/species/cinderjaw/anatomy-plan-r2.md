`anatomy-plan-r2.json` arrays. All unchanged parts, materials, child order,
species identity, collider, gameplay and six sibling species stay exact.

ankle ring and full-shell-proved upper cap. Add a wide lower knee atX±.54,

frontY.415/rearY.355, radii.235/.23. Growth stays within the original width.

an attached, visible sloped upper face.
