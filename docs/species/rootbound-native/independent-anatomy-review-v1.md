## Decision: CONDITIONAL PASS for the construction-reference gate

This sheet resolves the two reasons references v1 and v2 were held only when its complementary panels are read together. The front and rear elevations show only four unambiguous grounded feet (two per side); the remaining legs are occluded by the body and must not be counted from those views alone. The grounded feet, low saucer body, and large paired frond silhouette are consistent across the six views. The sheet is clean, opaque, free of environmental contamination and effects, and suitable as a construction plan for a single guarded mesh method.
