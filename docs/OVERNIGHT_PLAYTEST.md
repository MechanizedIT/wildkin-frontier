## A short five-minute check

1. **Jump at Camp.** Stand on the open apron and tap Jump, then try it while moving. On desktop use Space; R now dodges. The Explorer should rise and land once per press, with no double jump or continuing hops after holding the button. Orbit should still control the camera independently. New quiet movement sounds are a development candidate; report whether they are audible and pleasant on your phone.
2. **Explore above Forest Edge.** Travel out of Camp, pass the torn blue-gray Survey wreck through its open western side, then follow the eastern uphill route as it bends from the lower shelf to the high overlook. The height difference should be obvious, with room to turn and land. The high shelf holds a supply cache, and the lower route beside the warm gravel trail leads back down. A hidden wall, stuck movement on the visible path, floating loot or an unreadable player is a failure. You no longer need launch pads or checkpoints.
3. **Gather and reload.** On the lower eastern shelf, use the Omni-tool on a gray ore cluster with pale mineral faces. Collect one iron piece, stop safely and reload/Continue. The iron should stay in the backpack. Holding a harvest continues even when earlier drops are still on the ground; if an Auto Harvest drop lands beyond reach, walk toward it to collect its accumulated yield.

## Optional, only if you feel like exploring

- **Quiet western shore:** sneak near a Mossling with a clear view and wait for observation progress. Earned clues should remain readable in Pack → Journal; the dry bank should offer space to watch and place a lure. Being detected can interrupt observation.
- **Survey recovery:** enter the torn module from its open western side and open the chest. Its cartridge can be fitted at a placed Salvage bench for a permanent16→20-slot pack upgrade, using the displayed materials. A full pack should keep the reward available rather than lose it.
- **Defended Camp expansion:** clear the three marked debris bundles near the adjoining yard, then use its world console and displayed resource cost. The usable yard and emergency barricades should expand together and survive a reload. Existing placed storage/buildings should remain intact.
- **Rootfall passage:** the northern fallen growth has two cut seams and a brace/clear action using4wood+2fiber. Once cleared, the central lane should allow travel and a safe return; reloading should preserve the repair. Its current visual model is still under review, so an arch-like silhouette is a known art concern.

## What remains unfinished

Forest Edge is the first substantial terrain redesign; the other four expedition regions still need their distinct vertical layouts and discoveries. Its latest composition remains below the visual target: the next pass is one complete cliff/terrain/grass front, using the accepted V3 rocks, rather than a new rock family or more small moves. Rootfall's separate art direction and Tidefin's unshipped replacement remain held. Shatterfen's100×100m redesign is planning only. Camp drone/story progression, further defended sectors, powered/ranged weapons, broader blueprint progression and more creature/prop art remain planned.

Browser evidence establishes specific interactions, not sustained phone frame rate, heat, sound quality or whole-campaign balance. Short notes about what you actually see/hear/feel are more useful than trying every system.
