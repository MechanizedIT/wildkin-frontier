# Overnight prototype — player spot checks

September 11, 2026. This is an evolving local pre-alpha, not a release sign-off. Use landscape on your phone. Your existing save should remain available; do not clear it just to test.

**Current inventory cutover:** Pack is now the physical 16-slot backpack. From Pack, use **Journal** to reach **Gear**, **Work**, **Wildkin**, skills and Settings. Storage has 24 slots and must be opened beside the landing pod or a placed crate. Valid older saves migrate with retained overflow available through **Legacy supplies** at the pod. Keep a backup before testing; never delete your real save to manufacture a fresh run. The latest aggregate 810-test/build/ZIP checkpoint is 42.53 MB unpacked / 20.25 MB ZIP; root's final package/native closure follows the last Journal-entry repair, so this guide is not that acceptance receipt.

1. **Move and orbit.** From Camp, move toward the frontier gate. Drag on the right side while standing still; the camera should orbit without turning the Explorer. Press forward afterward; the Explorer should face and move along the camera heading. Walk/run/sneak are slower than the prior phone build. Report foot sliding or uncomfortable travel speed separately from pose quality.
2. **Equip supplies.** Open Pack → Journal → Gear. Tap an owned item, then a numbered slot to assign it. Only supplies physically in your backpack are usable; withdraw stored supplies first. Select a medkit on the bottom bar; its count should remain unchanged and the axe should disappear. After taking damage on an expedition, press Heal once or hold it; one medkit heals once, until you release and press again. Selecting the Omni-tool returns Attack/harvesting. Empty supply slots remain visible with zero stock.
3. **Build freely.** At Camp, follow the path south past the drop pod to the marked open clearing. Equip the construction blueprint and tap Build; choose a structure card. Its actual translucent model should appear. Drag the ground to position it, Rotate, then Place on green ground. Red means blocked/overlapping/outside the clearing and must not allow spending. A placed piece should match its preview; reload should keep it. Work can expand the current clearing using available physical materials. Keep the entrance path free. Planned defended sectors and automatic barricade expansion are not implemented by this clearing system.
4. **Mossling taming.** Craft a berry lure at Camp and equip it. Open Wildkin in the Journal: its habitat and complete guide should fit without scrolling in landscape. Go to the creek west of Verdant Lookout. Nudge the left stick to sneak (C with WASD on desktop), then use Place Berries from dry, open ground; a blocked placement should keep your lure. Retreat until Let It Approach appears, let it reach and eat the berries, then approach gently and Bond. A stationary animal that never reaches unobstructed food is a failure. Return to the Lookout and Extract to secure your new companion. Repeated Use during one attempt should not spend another lure. An earned desktop-browser journey passes; phone comfort and first-time discoverability still need your judgment.
5. **Other field methods.** Tidefin needs a baited dry-bank snare; Emberhorn needs you to dodge its committed charge before tethering; Skydancer needs a calming chime and space at its perches. Gear descriptions explain the first step. A full pending-capture slot should refuse another capture without spending supplies. These are new provisional mechanics and may need balance refinement.
6. **Wildlife and followers.** Sneak toward the creek's Mossling pair from behind, then rush toward one; a startled partner may briefly look and flee, while a quiet rear approach should leave both calm. A struck Mossling should show Wary and keep your bait. With a secured companion selected at Camp, walk, stop, turn sharply and circle an obstacle. It should choose a nearby resting position and catch up. The revised Mossling rig and gait still need your phone judgment: report sliding paws, shoulder/head distortion or animation cadence that does not match travel.
7. **World actions.** Near a portal, beacon or cache, the action button should point to the actual object and follow camera orbit. An incidental nearby Mossling should not hide Extract. Opening Pack hides in-world actions. A deliberately active taming attempt may take priority until it finishes or you cancel it.
8. **Explore the larger regions.** Look for winding side loops, creek/basin routes, higher approaches and return paths. Approach the outskirts and orbit the camera: visible terrain/obstacles should stop you before a raw map edge. Report invisible walls, disappearing ground, creature/resource overlaps and routes without useful destinations. The new perimeter rock art and sparse outer destinations are still being refined.

9. **Physical crafting.** In the Camp clearing, place a Salvage bench, Matter fabricator or Resonance bench. Stand beside it and tap its floating button. Choose a recipe and Craft: the vise, nozzle or crystal assembly should move, then show a seated product. Opening Pack mid-cycle pauses the machine; closing it resumes. Walking away dismisses the picker. Reload must keep packed counts and the station, without granting a second product. A second nearby machine should stay idle. The tiny chime on the deck is easier to identify from its recipe icon.

10. **Recognize and harvest Sapwood.** Near Verdant's first path, find the pale fan of woody ribs beside the berry bush and grey rock. With Auto off, strike five times: each hit removes one complete rib with its leaves, leaving a low cut root. Stand on the root until it regrows, then step away. It should not shove or trap you; collision returns once you are clear. Returning on another expedition should show the full plant normally. Check its appearance from behind as well.
11. **Open a physical chest.** Find a chest beside a trail's finish flag. Collect it and watch its catches release before the lid lifts. Open Pack during the motion, close it, then reload. It should pause/resume, remain open after reload and never repeat the reward before refill. A Mossling-sealed cache first needs your companion's ability. The Ember Forge Vault has its own shutters/tray; wait for it to finish opening before collecting.
12. **Pack a Trail ration.** At a placed Salvage bench, craft a ration using two Berries and one Fiber from your pack or selected nearby storage. Leave room for its output. Assign it through Pack → Journal → Gear. During an expedition, take nonlethal damage, select the ration and tap Eat. It restores one health and consumes one ration; selecting it or using it at full health keeps the stock. Holding Use or opening Pack must not repeatedly consume food.
13. **Walk beneath the alien canopy.** Beside Camp and Verdant's paths, find the broad green paddle-leaf trees. The standard canopy has a blocking trunk and walkable space below its crown; tall/spread variants retain their existing non-solid scenery contract. Orbit until a tree is between the camera and Explorer: the whole tree should fade enough to keep the Explorer readable, then return opaque when the view clears. Nearby trees should not fade together. Harvestable Sapwood keeps its different woody-rib shape.

14. **Watch the Fen receiver.** At Shatterfen’s observatory, approach the blue extraction waypoint in front of the octagonal instrument. Discovery should start a slow bowl tilt while both supports stay fixed. Orbit to its side to see the motion. Pack should freeze it; reduced motion in Settings should hold it still. You should still be able to walk to the blue waypoint and return to Camp. Floating supports, a clipped moving bowl or an obstructed extraction route are failures.

15. **Prepare physical storage.** Stand beside the landing pod and tap Pod locker. Select a stack and use To Backpack/To Pod locker, or drag between slots; split a stack and sort one container. Totals should stay unchanged, and the medkit toolbar count should fall when medkits leave the pack. Close and walk away: Pack should still open, but remote storage transfer must be unavailable. At a placed crate, repeat only if you already have one; it is separate storage, not a second view of the pod. Legacy supplies are withdrawal-only and should explain both the ready stack and remaining quantity. A missing reachable locker label, disappearing items, duplicate counts or movement while the panel is open are failures.

16. **Resume and return.** Export a backup in Settings. On an ordinary expedition, collect a small affordable stack, stop on safe supported ground and reload. The saved pack, region, supported position, health, carried XP and pending bonds should resume without repeating rewards; ordinary creatures/loose drops are rebuilt. Extract at a known waypoint: packed materials should stay packed, XP/new bonds become secured, and storage changes only when you deposit. Death provisionally keeps the pack but loses carried XP and pending bonds. Do not deliberately sacrifice an important pending bond just to test this rule.

Known limits: the Wildkin bed remains decorative and needs its own art/companion-rest pass. Powered/ranged weapons, backpack upgrades, blueprints, new wreckage progression and defended Camp sections are pending. The new Tidefin V3 animation study is not shipping. Much of the broader zone composition remains under review, including artificial-looking boundary walls. Browser checks cannot establish your phone's sustained frame rate, temperature or comfort.
