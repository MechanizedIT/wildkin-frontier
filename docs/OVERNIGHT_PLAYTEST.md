# Overnight prototype — player spot checks

September 11, 2026. This is an evolving local pre-alpha, not a release sign-off. Use landscape on your phone. Your existing save should remain available; do not clear it just to test.

1. **Move and orbit.** From Camp, move toward the frontier gate. Drag on the right side while standing still; the camera should orbit without turning the Explorer. Press forward afterward; the Explorer should face and move along the camera heading. Walk/run/sneak are slower than the prior phone build. Report foot sliding or uncomfortable travel speed separately from pose quality.
2. **Equip supplies.** Open Pack → Equipment. Tap an owned item, then a numbered slot to assign it. Select a medkit on the bottom bar; its count should remain unchanged and the axe should disappear. After taking damage on an expedition, press Heal once or hold it; one medkit heals once, until you release and press again. Selecting the Omni-tool returns Attack/harvesting. Empty supply slots remain visible with zero stock.
3. **Build freely.** At Camp, follow the path south past the drop pod to the marked open clearing. Equip the construction blueprint and tap Build; choose a structure card. Its actual translucent model should appear. Drag the ground to position it, Rotate, then Place on green ground. Red means blocked/overlapping/outside the clearing and must not allow spending. A placed piece should match its preview; reload should keep it. The Work panel can expand the clearing with secured resources. Keep the entrance path free.
4. **Mossling taming.** Craft a berry lure at Camp. In Verdant Verge, equip it and approach a Mossling. Place the lure, then retreat to give it room to feed. Follow the compact guide and approach quietly when invited. Movement and camera should remain usable. Bonding is unsecured until you extract. Repeated Use during the same attempt should not spend another lure.
5. **Other field methods.** Tidefin needs a baited dry-bank snare; Emberhorn needs you to dodge its committed charge before tethering; Skydancer needs a calming chime and space at its perches. Gear descriptions explain the first step. A full pending-capture slot should refuse another capture without spending supplies. These are new provisional mechanics and may need balance refinement.
6. **Wildlife and followers.** Sneak toward the creek's Mossling pair from behind, then rush toward one; a startled partner may briefly look and flee, while a quiet rear approach should leave both calm. A struck Mossling should show Wary and keep your bait. With a secured companion selected at Camp, walk, stop, turn sharply and circle an obstacle. It should choose a nearby resting position and catch up. The revised Mossling rig and gait still need your phone judgment: report sliding paws, shoulder/head distortion or animation cadence that does not match travel.
7. **World actions.** Near a portal, beacon or cache, the action button should point to the actual object and follow camera orbit. An incidental nearby Mossling should not hide Extract. Opening Pack hides in-world actions. A deliberately active taming attempt may take priority until it finishes or you cancel it.
8. **Explore the larger regions.** Look for winding side loops, creek/basin routes, higher approaches and return paths. Approach the outskirts and orbit the camera: visible terrain/obstacles should stop you before a raw map edge. Report invisible walls, disappearing ground, creature/resource overlaps and routes without useful destinations. The new perimeter rock art and sparse outer destinations are still being refined.

9. **Physical crafting.** In the Camp clearing, place a Salvage bench, Matter fabricator or Resonance bench. Stand beside it and tap its floating button. Choose a recipe and Craft: the vise, nozzle or crystal assembly should move, then show a seated product. Opening Pack mid-cycle pauses the machine; closing it resumes. Walking away dismisses the picker. Reload must keep packed counts and the station, without granting a second product. A second nearby machine should stay idle. The tiny chime on the deck is easier to identify from its recipe icon.

10. **Recognize and harvest Sapwood.** Near Verdant's first path, find the pale fan of woody ribs beside the berry bush and grey rock. With Auto off, strike five times: each hit removes one complete rib with its leaves, leaving a low cut root. Stand on the root until it regrows, then step away. It should not shove or trap you; collision returns once you are clear. Returning on another expedition should show the full plant normally. Check its appearance from behind as well.
11. **Open a physical chest.** Find a chest beside a trail's finish flag. Collect it and watch its catches release before the lid lifts. Open Pack during the motion, close it, then reload. It should pause/resume, remain open after reload and never repeat the reward before refill. A Mossling-sealed cache first needs your companion's ability. The Ember Forge Vault has its own shutters/tray; wait for it to finish opening before collecting.
12. **Pack a Trail ration.** At a placed Salvage bench, craft a ration using two secured Berries and one Fiber. Assign it to a quick slot in Pack. During an expedition, take nonlethal damage, select the ration and tap Eat. It restores one health and consumes one ration; selecting it or using it at full health keeps the stock. Holding Use or opening Pack must not repeatedly consume food.

Known limits: the Wildkin bed remains decorative and needs its own art/companion-rest pass. Powered/ranged weapons are still pending. Much of the broader zone composition remains under review, including artificial-looking boundary walls. Browser checks cannot establish your phone's sustained frame rate, temperature or comfort.
