- Reference author, asset builder and independent judge remain separate roles. Reference, model, motion and runtime admission remain distinct gates; a conditional verdict stays conditional until its condition is tested.

- Keep at most three production writers, a reserved independent reviewer, and one heavy GPU job at a time. The active continuous rotation supersedes the historical three-batch/session ceiling. Each individual visit still has its own three-pass cap.
