# Early alpha: Wildkin make the frontier useful

September 13,2026. Chris's current direction is to reach an early alpha as soon as practical while continuing the procedural world, habitats, Wildkin and core mechanics. His proposed world shape is one seeded continent surrounded by ocean, with boats potentially opening larger continents later. This producer plan adopts that bounded-continent direction; it is not yet a promised release extent or an infinite-world claim. Likewise, about 10 habitats and roughly 30 useful species is a staged continent target, not the first-alpha content count.

## Release promise and core loop

A new player can complete a useful 10–15 minute portrait outing: notice a physical Camp need, choose a risky habitat, learn and bond with a Wildkin, use its recognizable ability to secure resources or survive the return, then improve Camp. Map state, resources, depleted sources and exact Wildkin survive reopening.

The first useful Mossling loop is now connected: earned supplies→lure/bond→physical return→bed/feed/garden→four-berry harvest, with saved continuation. The new purpose reads those real needs and the inventory HUD follows committed spending. [Actual journey and limits](../art/reviews/alpha-outing/receipt.md). Portrait construction framing is the immediate release gap: the garden needed a landscape placement workaround. Fix that existing interaction next, then give the procedural frontier more repeated companion jobs. A generic quest or reward ledger would describe the loop without making it fun.

## What the first Wildkin already do

These are current code facts, separate from proposed expansion:

| Wildkin | Current field and Camp effect | Early-alpha utility role to make repeatable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mossling | **Bloom** heals 2 health on a 24-second cooldown and opens its legacy root seal. A fully nourished settled Mossling within 6 m of a Berry Garden raises a ready crop from 3 to 4 berries. Its completed field notes can guide one parent's body tone in breeding. | Recovery and cultivation: the companion for longer forage outings, living-root access and a visibly stronger food loop. Extend through seeded opportunities using the existing heal/seal/garden contracts, not a second farming system. |
| Tidefin | **Tidal Ward** grants 3 seconds of invulnerability on a 26-second cooldown and opens its legacy tidal seal. Observation teaches its existing snare-and-release bond. | Protection and safe approach: the companion for a clearly telegraphed short hazard or guarded resource window. Prove the ward changes what the player can safely do; do not imply swimming or water traversal. |
| Emberhorn | **Cragbreaker** damages nearby creatures within 4.5 m for 3.5× the current field-tool damage multiplier on an 18-second cooldown and opens its legacy mineral seal. Observation teaches its existing warn/charge/recovery/tether bond. | Breaking and defense: the companion for reinforced mineral access, blocking debris or a dangerous resource pocket. Prefer one physical break/access contract over expanding combat. |

The abilities and legacy seals exist; the repeatable procedural-world jobs in the last column are proposals. A species earns admission through a distinct silhouette or body structure, recognizable movement, and a useful habitat/player role. Body-tone differences remain individual variation, not new species. Shared rigs, animations and modular parts are welcome when the creature still reads and plays differently.

## Admission and exit checks

- Keep current ceilings until measurement earns a change:25 published terrain chunks plus at most nine prepared candidates; four live wildlife residents with at most one regional-signature resident;12 forage sources per chunk; and8,192 saved frontier-resource records.
- Scenery's `maxTotal:34` is the global live selection cap for the current terrain residency, not a per-chunk allowance. Its current selection also caps near scenery at18, desired outer canopies at8 and total canopies at12. Major place families should share a provisional combined ceiling of one large whole group in any 3×3 live chunk window.
- Record worst-case meshes, colliders, triangles, source records and construction time for every admitted habitat/species recipe. Prefer regional replacement over additive populations.
- A fresh player can understand and complete one need-driven outing without coordinates: recognize the habitat, observe/bond or select the useful Wildkin, see its ability change the outcome, return and receive a visible Camp benefit.
- Atlas, outing, inventory, exact individual identity, partial depletion, care/garden/breeding and rewards survive literal reload. Failed saves remain atomic and retryable.
- The tested route has supported collision/resume, readable danger and bounded residents. Recurrent input freezes at boundaries are release blockers.
- The offline ZIP cold-starts from local vendored resources, has a clean console and passes one aggregate/package gate. Record size and actual performance evidence.
- A physical portrait phone must verify controls, text, loading, memory and frame pacing before claiming a tested mobile alpha; landscape/desktop receive a smoke pass.

## Scope and review

Defer coast implementation until companion payoff works unless safety requires it. Also defer swimming, boats, additional continents, rivers, weather/day-night, cave families, universal climbing, a large new roster, new eye/marking systems, deep genetics, DNA/cloning/trade, seed selection, accounts and backend work. Do not reopen held scenery/province art unless the intended habitat, obstacle or ability cannot be read in ordinary portrait play.

Use one baseline and a concrete player-visible target, one implementation capture, one independent go/no-go and at most one alpha-critical correction pass. Run focused proof while developing, one real affected journey, and one aggregate/package gate per integrated playable batch. Save atomicity, physics/support, determinism/caps and offline correctness remain blockers; record nonblocking polish debt and continue.
