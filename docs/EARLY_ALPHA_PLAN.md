## Release promise and core loop

A new player can complete a useful 10–15 minute portrait outing: notice a physical Camp need, choose a risky habitat, learn and bond with a Wildkin, use its recognizable ability to secure resources or survive the return, then improve Camp. Map state, resources, depleted sources and exact Wildkin survive reopening.

## Admission and exit checks

- Keep current ceilings until measurement earns a change:25 published terrain chunks plus at most nine prepared candidates; four live wildlife residents with at most one regional-signature resident;12 forage sources per chunk; and8,192 saved frontier-resource records.
- Scenery's `maxTotal:34` is the global live selection cap for the current terrain residency, not a per-chunk allowance. Its current selection also caps near scenery at18, desired outer canopies at8 and total canopies at12. Major place families should share a provisional combined ceiling of one large whole group in any 3×3 live chunk window.
- Record worst-case meshes, colliders, triangles, source records and construction time for every admitted habitat/species recipe. Prefer regional replacement over additive populations.
- A fresh player can understand and complete one need-driven outing without coordinates: recognize the habitat, observe/bond or select the useful Wildkin, see its ability change the outcome, return and receive a visible Camp benefit.
- Atlas, outing, inventory, exact individual identity, partial depletion, care/garden/breeding and rewards survive literal reload. Failed saves remain atomic and retryable.
- The tested route has supported collision/resume, readable danger and bounded residents. Recurrent input freezes at boundaries are release blockers.
- The offline ZIP cold-starts from local vendored resources, has a clean console and passes one aggregate/package gate. Record size and actual performance evidence.
- A physical portrait phone must verify controls, text, loading, memory and frame pacing before claiming a tested mobile alpha; landscape/desktop receive a smoke pass.

## Scope and review

Keep the implemented coast and surface-swim foundation bounded while its R3 visual admission is reviewed. Defer boats, diving, richer coasts, additional continents, rivers, weather/day-night, cave families, universal climbing, a large new roster, new eye/marking systems, deep genetics, DNA/cloning/trade, seed selection, accounts and backend work. Do not reopen held scenery/province art unless an intended habitat, obstacle or ability cannot be read in ordinary portrait play.

Use one baseline and a concrete player-visible target, one implementation capture, one independent go/no-go and at most one alpha-critical correction pass. Run focused proof while developing, one real affected journey, and one aggregate/package gate per integrated playable batch. Save atomicity, physics/support, determinism/caps and offline correctness remain blockers; record nonblocking polish debt and continue.

The scoped hybrid workflow remains a provisional producer choice after two trial batches. It does not change the global Wildkin, Dream Loop or Rapid Alpha Producer skills; retain it only while its bounded ownership, focused review and single-gate pattern continues to help this project.
