# Fungal Hollow — next native blockout

September 13, 2026. Provisional engineering plan under the continuous early-alpha goal. This is not an implemented habitat or a new production contract.

The next visible improvement should be a recognizably fungal place, using low mushroom crescents, fallen wood and solid stone toes to give the ground shape and density. Keep a useful discovery/gathering destination and an escape route. A new view already exposes more scenery; prove the composition at that actual 36° / 11.591m portrait camera before adding another terrain profile.

Two read-only audits found a practical starting point:

- The fixed Fungal center at (-2850,-1250) is fully inland and strongly inside this habitat. A sampled 500×500m neighborhood is dry land with pure Fungal ownership. A south approach has mild existing relief and room for an authored hollow; this is sampled design evidence, not physical traversal proof.
- The admitted kit includes mushroom rings, fallen logs, Fen stones, trail stones, pebbles, a luminous blossom resource and a Thornprowler visual/actor. Mooncap mushrooms are only about 1.04m high at scale1, so a generated towering canopy would overpromise. Start with uneven crescents at credible scale and terrain-backed clusters. Fen stones have a solid core; soft mushrooms/logs/pebbles are nonblocking under their current owners.
- One estimated 23-prop low kit costs roughly20k visible triangles and5–10 instance draws in two render cells. A resource and live creature add separate work; verify the compiled kit and real frame rather than relying on this estimate.

Root will select one near approach, inspect complete transformed bounds and blocked-ground footprints, then create a native rough composition. A coherent whole-habitat concave terrain grammar can follow only after that visible structure and ordinary approach work.

Avoid copying the Caldera's mineral arrangement or introducing another creature-specific cache key merely for novelty. The audit's Tidefin-locked cache is not selected. Reuse ordinary gathering/discovery and a clearly avoidable existing actor where useful; an existing actor does not count as a new fungal species. The wider species roster and its distinctive roles remain a separate alpha requirement.

Detailed working audits remain at `.dream-loop/next-habitat/fungal-kit-audit.md` and `fungal-place-audit.md`. `CURRENT_SLICE.md` is authoritative. No assets were downloaded, dependencies added, resident budgets expanded or Fungal runtime content changed in this checkpoint.
