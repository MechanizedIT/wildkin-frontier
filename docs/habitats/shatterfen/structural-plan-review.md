## Correction after full-geometry/HUD audit

**Source implementation status: HOLD.** The finite proposal is still suitable for a reversible native visual preview, but not for source placement. Its `clear(x,z)` calculation subtracts source radii and the 1.2 m route corridor from the proposed **center** without subtracting each moved lily/reed's scaled hull radius. The stated 1.718–2.918 m clearances therefore do not establish full-hull source/route clearance. In addition, proposed projected centers around y=180–208 overlap the actual destination observation-banner zone (about y=174–245), so center-in-frame is not useful portrait visibility.

Before source freeze, use full existing scaled/yawed render hulls for all four records against sources and route, and require useful non-HUD projection coverage for their actual meshes. Keep the exact four-record limit, their noncolliding-only status, all wildlife terrain/physics protections, and no terrain layout revival. A reversible preview may resolve the visual question but cannot substitute for those placement proofs.
