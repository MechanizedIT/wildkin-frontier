# Fungal Hollow structural plan V2 — independent review

## Decision: CONDITIONAL PASS for full-footprint proof and an isolated native mesh preview only

V2 fixes the V1 search limitation. It uses the recorded destination portrait camera and a finite 2 m survey, then selects a materially larger screen-adjacent candidate rather than treating three off-screen lobe centers as a feasibility result. The `(-2840, -1289)` low ellipse is small and bounded (`6 × 5 m`, `.55 m` peak), avoids listed hard identity disks, and its post-addition projected vertices span roughly `x=198..365`, `y=165..246`.

This is enough evidence to spend a reversible preview step. It is not approval for a source mutation or a claim of visual success. The seven projected vertices describe potential placement, not the perceived silhouette, and the top of the stated range remains HUD-sensitive.

The 138 route-interior triangle check (maximum slope `.169643`, none over `.32`) is useful but incomplete. Before any source pass, the candidate must prove the full 3 m supported corridor width on the final rendered terrain, actual `.32 m` player footprint traversal/clearance, all resource and solid-stone fitted supports, the Thorn disk plus feather, each blossom's intended support including the deliberately shelf-grounded `#402`, sampler/cap parity, native persistence, and a native preview that is perceptually useful in the locked pose.

The contract audit correctly distinguishes the source's smooth preservation algorithm from the owner-locked requirement: supported width/passability is mandatory; a generic zero-height-delta rule is not established. No route, stable identity, home, stone fit, source, or cap may be changed to make this candidate fit.
