# Fungal Hollow structural plan V1 — independent review

## Decision: HOLD — planning evidence only

The V1 material correctly leaves source frozen. Its reconstructed destination view is internally consistent with the recorded portrait camera, but the proposed finite candidate is not a perceptual solution: the only useful projected mesh point is only about 0.023 m of relief at `(352.3, 173.6)`. That lies at the top edge of the proposed usable rectangle and remains vulnerable to the real dynamic HUD. It does not establish a visible, continuous pocket shoulder.

The three guessed lobe centers being off-screen does not establish that no feasible visible envelope exists. The requested one finite, screen-aware search is the appropriate next planning step. It must use the actual native destination camera and current HUD, project the whole proposed mesh/triangles rather than isolated centers, and then prove the resulting candidate against the contracts below before any source pass.

## Contract findings

- The fixed Fungal config, branch/bounds data, stable blossom identities and transforms, Thorn home and its full protected radius/feather, resource records, dressing/stones, sampler order, caps, and route records remain exact. No resource, home, wildlife, or collider change is authorized.
- The certified approach and return corridors need their **full supported width and passability** preserved against the final terrain, rather than only a center-line or direct-anchor sample. Solid Fen stones and resource supports likewise need fitted footprint/support checks, not center-point clearances.
- The current `routePreservation()` smooth mask is source behavior, not evidence that the owner-locked rule is simply “direct height equals zero.” It includes supported width, mesh margin, and a transition, while later terrain processing also applies route-safe blending. A proposal must compare original and final terrain over actual capsule/footprint samples on the 2 m terrain grid and demonstrate support/passability.
- `blossomPreservation()` deliberately exempts blossom `f1:b:402` because it is grounded on the raised shelf. The proof’s direct zero samples for every identity are therefore not the authoritative constraint. The three normally protected blossoms must retain their intended smooth supported envelopes; `#402` must retain its intended raised-shelf support and transform rather than be forced to a generic zero-height rule.
- The Thorn exclusion is correctly understood as its complete home disk plus feather. Outer relief itself need not be walkable; the protected corridor and required supports do.

## Evidence limits

The ordinary existing-pocket journey (`121.693 s`, `191.714 m`, three wildflowers, health `5 → 5`, exact reload, no errors) is useful baseline evidence only. It neither proves a three-to-five-minute circuit nor validates the proposed structural composition.

## Required next gate

After the single finite search, retain only candidates with a material projected bank/shoulder outside the actual HUD, then supply native full-mesh projection plus full-width route, source/stone/resource footprint, blossom, and Thorn-home final-terrain proofs. A fresh independent plan and source review is required before mutation.
