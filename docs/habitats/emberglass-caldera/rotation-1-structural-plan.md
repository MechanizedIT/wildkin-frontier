# Emberglass Caldera — rotation 1 structural plan

**Status: proposed for independent technical review; no source authorization.** This is one terrain-first composition operation for the selected `interior-connected-shoulders` direction.

## Bounded form and negative space

The operation adds two unequal, smooth shoulder lobes to the existing Caldera profile, not scenery or a new terrain system:

| Lobe | Center | Radii X/Z | Peak | Intended role |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| left connected shoulder | (845.2, -1968.5) | 2.4 m / 5.5 m | +1.10 m | stronger left breach edge that turns toward the rear |
| right broken shoulder | (854.8, -1969) | 2.3 m / 5 m | +0.72 m | lower opposing edge, leaving the charcoal center open |

Each uses the continuous `peak * (1 - n²)²` ellipse inside its footprint. It remains zero in the central `|x-850| <= 2.32` breach corridor from z -1980 through -1932, throughout each mineral footprint, and across the full Emberhorn 10 m refuge. The operation therefore keeps an at-least-4.64 m ungraded central travel strip while the eventual implementation must still prove the certified full 4 m corridor and .32 m player footprint on final 2 m triangles.

This locates the rise in the only finite interior screen band that clears the center floor. It deliberately does not put a wide shoulder at x839/x861: those forms project outside the narrow portrait view and would recreate the earlier off-screen-anchor failure.
