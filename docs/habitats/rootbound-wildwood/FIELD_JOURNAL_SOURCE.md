# Rootbound dossiers as a future field-journal source

Owner direction, September 14: the habitat and constituent dossiers should also supply illustrated player-facing knowledge, including individual Wildkin entries. This is a content-authoring contract; no journal system or unlock mechanic is implemented here.

## One fact source, several presentations

Keep a stable identity for each habitat, subhabitat, species, material and discovery. Link entries by those identities. Separate the observed or designed fact from its player-facing wording so a future journal, tooltip and production brief do not develop contradictory copies. Existing runtime IDs must be verified before mapping a dossier entry to them; proposed names are not save IDs.

Provisional authoring keys use `rootbound.<kind>.<slug>`, for example `rootbound.subhabitat.root-galleries`. Record content status (`proposed`, `implemented`, `verified`), audience state (`draft`, `designer-only`, `player-ready`), and evidence links separately. These are document metadata, not a new runtime schema. Each practical player claim needs a concrete source or gameplay receipt before becoming player-ready; a linked portrait capture supports visual identification, not unseen behavior. Keep secret details in the designer-only appendix and publish only the intended discovery layer.

Each dossier should include:

| Content | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Identity and status | Stable dossier key, display-name candidate, habitat/parent links, proposed versus implemented status |
| Illustrated field entry | Short evocative description, recognizable silhouette, scale reference, habitat and visible identification cues |
| Practical observations | Where it occurs, what it does, relevant resource uses or creature behavior, grounded in verified gameplay when presented as player advice |
| Ecological relationships | Climate/moisture design cues, subhabitat distribution, food/shelter associations and linked constituents |
| Discovery layers | Ordinary knowledge, information intended to be learned through observation, and designer-only secrets; unlock conditions remain proposals |
| Production appendix | Dimensions, modeling plan, asset paths, camera poses, collision/streaming costs, source evidence and review findings |
| Visual provenance | Image role, source/prompt, review status, revision, and whether it depicts a concept or actual game content |

## Publication boundary

A compelling concept is not automatically true in the game. Player-facing claims about taming, danger, drops, crafting equivalence or discovery rewards need a matching implemented rule and evidence before publication. Climate descriptions can establish fictional setting without implying a temperature simulation. Keep unimplemented mechanics and technical review scores out of player prose.

Keep high-resolution source illustrations and editable text. Derive smaller journal images later; preserve an accessible text description and useful silhouette at small display size. Species construction sheets, scenic targets and journal illustrations serve different purposes and should be explicitly labeled rather than treating one image as suitable for everything.

## Rootbound example — proposed field prose

**Root Galleries:** “Broad roots gather into sheltered passages beneath the woodland. Look for openings in the root walls: brighter ground marks the clearing edges, while fallen wood and small fungi gather in the shade.”

This is draft environmental prose, not a claim that the retained game already delivers every cue. Once the composition is implemented, verify those cues in ordinary portrait play before promoting the entry to player-ready content. Avoid revealing hidden discoveries in the ordinary habitat description.

## Review responsibilities

An independent design reviewer checks coherent geography, ecology and useful player knowledge. An art reviewer checks identity, composition and visual consistency. A modeling reviewer checks dimensions, connections, bends/taper, materials and a practical construction route. Runtime review then confirms that player-facing statements match actual behavior. These are review perspectives assigned to agents, not claims of human professional credentials.

Approve content and implementation separately. A well-written dossier can be ready for production while its journal entry remains unpublished and its asset remains a candidate.
