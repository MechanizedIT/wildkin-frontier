# Rootbound Wildwood dossier — independent coherence review

## Verdict: strong working design; ready to guide constituent reference work

The dossier now gives Rootbound a coherent five-part sequence: Orientation Meadow is the safe reset; Root Galleries provide the raised root corridor; Lantern Grove supplies damp sheltered discovery; Thornstone Verge is the exposed caution edge; and Heartroot Crown forms the warm return-facing landmark. The moisture, light, and material gradient makes that sequence legible without inventing a weather simulation. The material table also correctly keeps regional wood, food, stone, and fiber names as proposed presentation over existing shared categories.

The actual/proposed distinction is mostly sound. Implemented terrain, clearing palette, curated scenery, retained grove, route protections, Mossling, and Bloom are identified as retained evidence. Climate values, local names, landmark identity, discoverables, Galleryfall/Lanterncap/Verge/Crownfiber presentation, Trailgloam, and future assets remain explicitly proposed. The Crown section correctly withdraws any unsupported assertion that it is already the earned grove.

## Critical additions before production expands

1. Add one compact **subzone-to-circuit map/table** that links each of the five subhabitats to its packet-owned ordinary comparison pose, protected lane/home content, and one visible camera-facing anchor. The dossier describes transitions well, but does not yet make a placement or projection claim reviewable.
2. Turn the visual backlog into a **priority queue with quality contracts**: root anchor first; then damp log/cap cluster, thornstone seam, Heartroot collar, and Trailgloam. Each row should name its normal portrait role, required support/reference views, approximate scale band, existing-kit match or explicit asset gap, and whether it is a construction target or a runtime candidate. This prevents a broad tree image from being treated as a substitute for the connected root anchor.
3. Add a short **cross-zone material-family matrix** tying rootwood, humus/log/fungi, thornstone, and Crown litter to permitted palette/value ranges and the existing asset families. This will keep the five zones related while preventing a uniform brown-green prop mix.

No dossier text should promote the proposed local labels into item IDs, UI strings, new drops, discovery state, climate simulation, or a Trailgloam encounter before the separately scoped source and runtime evidence exists.
