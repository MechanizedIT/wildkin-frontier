## Journey hypothesis

Latest [exploration steering](TRAVERSAL_INTENT.md) allows optional climbing and harvestable-clearing pockets. This centerline describes a main route hypothesis, not a demand for walkability everywhere. Focus proof on the actual main outing, intentional climb/clear transitions, critical interactions and traps; do not run exhaustive ordinary-walk validation for each art edit. The broad decor-destruction and emergency-escape ideas are future proposals.

The historical local trace is the evidence baseline; it is not restated as a new timing result. This plan hypothesizes a 3–5 minute outing only after a measured ordinary loop: Arrival → Galleries → Lantern Grove → Crown → Meadow, with optional Verge branch. Interactions and the branch must supply duration; no idle laps. Required proof: actual 2m/player-footprint route, all source/home parity, asset hull/support, ordinary walk, persistence, and matching captures.

## Ecotones

Meadow→Galleries: brighter litter to root shade. Galleries→Grove: dry rib to fallen wood/humus. Grove→Verge: humus to mineral seam. Crown is a sheltered branch of Galleries, not a claimed existing grove. These gradients are visual/ecological direction; no climate simulation is proposed.

Use irregular overlapping margins rather than hard boundaries. Outbound and west-return views should reveal different faces of shared anchors. The map plots35current life-preservation points, three stable-plate areas, three ridge polylines, hollow and thorn extents and recorded cameras; it is not a survey of every obstacle or home footprint. Final-triangle support, source/home/resource continuity, complete asset hulls, ordinary gathering and travel, streaming and exact reload remain open. Current mask algorithms do not automatically lock all terrain heights, but their supported-life outcomes must remain proven.
