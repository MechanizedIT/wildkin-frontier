## Rapier measurement correction

Three repeated runs of the vendored Rapier 0.20 runtime returned exactly **0.153238 rad** of rotation after 300 steps, while moving the boulder more than 6 m. The old `>0.25 rad` threshold disagreed with both those deterministic runs and the earlier browser observation (~0.19 rad). The assertion now checks meaningful translation and rotation (`>0.12 rad`), finite poses, sleep after 600 total steps, stable settled position, near-zero velocity, and targetable/mineable matter at the moved pose. No proxy shape or physics tuning was changed.

## Human retest

Open [`lab/voxel/cellular-mixed.html?fixture=buried`](../lab/voxel/cellular-mixed.html?fixture=buried) in the repository's local server. Drag to orbit around the large soil mound, then click the visible soil repeatedly at the crosshair. The same surface should expose an angular stone patch while the rock count stays unchanged until a direct strike on visible stone. Keep digging at the dirt bank beneath the rock until it drops; follow it with **F**, wait for it to settle, and click its visible face to mine. Use **Save & reload** and confirm the cavity and moved actor return. Fail signs: dirt vanishes while targeted stone changes, the detached actor stays behind in its old spot, two apparent pieces move as one after a cut, dirt floats unsupported after the fall, unrelated dirt mining wakes or shifts a settled actor, or reload restores rock inside the cleared cavity.

## Limits and disposition

This is a 13³ isolated proof using approximate convex proxies and one bounded Surface Nets field. It does not demonstrate a material seam across a logical chunk boundary, streaming, production integration, the PC performance target, or the mobile-landscape budget. Dirt support is deliberately bounded to this fixture. HYBRID generation-time source composition with resolved runtime matter remains a provisional recommendation. **C.1R closes its listed remediation; Phase 0.5D remains unstarted and requires fresh owner direction.**
