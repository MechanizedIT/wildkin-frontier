# A living frontier in your pocket

September12,2026. Chris woke during F7A and requested an update, investigation of Honeycomb, and a mobile/casual-first direction with portrait play and simple intuitive controls. This supersedes the earlier PC/web-first and landscape-first priorities. Portrait is the primary design target; desktop/landscape remain useful supported layouts. Existing danger/exploration/creature-family goals remain.

## Positioning proposal

Discover a creature, bring it home, and shape its family. Build the appeal around five connected experiences:

- Short useful outings inside a persistent mapped frontier. Aim for one satisfying clue, encounter or resource decision in5–10minutes; longer trips remain possible. This timing is a design target, not measured current pacing.
- Individual companions with recognizable inherited appearance, family history and useful habits. A lineage should matter emotionally and in exploration, without requiring genetics spreadsheets.
- A personal atlas that remembers places and invites sharing discoveries. Later field/DNA cards can carry provenance; offline copying is not verified scarce trading.
- A physical Camp of beds, gardens, habitats and research tools. Show needs and changes in the world, with short nearby actions.
- Readable danger and preparation. Safe Camp and early routes support casual play; deeper excursions demand choices. No punishment for missing days, mandatory old-age death, or claim that casual means every place is safe.

## What Honeycomb establishes

The likely TikTok game is Honeycomb: The World Beyond by Frozen Way. Its official pitch already includes alien biomes, survival/exploration, flora/fauna, base laboratories, crafting and crossbreeding plants/animals. Those ideas alone do not distinguish Wildkin. The September8 publisher announcement lists Steam, Epic, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The sources reviewed do not establish procedural generation or a mobile release; neither should be claimed absent merely because unverified.

Sources reviewed September12: [official game](https://honeycomb-game.com/), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1510440/Honeycomb/), [publisher launch](https://snail.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/snail-games-announces-global-launch-honeycomb-world-beyond). The official site retained an older Coming2026 banner; the dated publisher announcement is more current for release status.

Mobile collecting/breeding is also established by [Pocket Frogs](https://nimblebit.com/) and [DragonVale](https://webshop.dragonvale.decagames.com/). Wildkin's proposed identity combines approachable creature-family collecting with direct physical exploration, personal mapping and contextual care. This is positioning judgment, not proof of an unoccupied market or exhaustive competitor playtesting.

## M1 portrait controls decision

F7A and M1 are complete. Portrait now uses a fixed movement stick, stable higher camera, one large contextual action, compact traversal/escape controls and a small equipped-tool/Pack surface. A tap on the equipped item opens the existing selectable quick-slot belt in portrait; Pack remains the physical inventory. The Pack shortcut editor only assigns slots, so it cannot replace equipping. Desktop shortcuts remain available.

The selected camera keeps yaw stable and preserves camera-relative movement. World actions retain physical range and visibility rules, with a marker linking the thumb button to its target. Pointer ownership, held-action cancellation, modal/Author behavior, danger and the single loop remain intact. Native portrait Plant/Feed/Jump/travel and landscape regression checks passed; prior F7A evidence covers Pair and portrait Welcome. M1's final visual review scored9.0/10. Emulation is layout/input proof; physical-phone comfort, load time, memory and frame pacing still require an actual device. Exact evidence: `art/reviews/living-frontier-m1/receipt.md`.
