# Independent U4F-C1 construction-plan review

Disposition: **acceptable to execute one cleanup pass**, subject to a hard visual stop.

The reviewer agreed that the defect distribution and fragmented boundary graph justify one voxel reconstruction instead of automatic boundary filling. A lower-band, irregular support-core is a suitable way to replace the underside cavity without a flat cap.

The initial plan's 0.64 normalized core height was flagged as too tall: it could intrude into the lower terraces and round the approved form. Before construction, the plan was revised using the read-only underside ray probe: the core now terminates at normalized Z 0.33, just above the highest sampled underside first-hit of 0.286. Its broad rings taper by 0.24. The plan now requires matched-view and silhouette/landmark review of the closed high-resolution working solid before any decimation, and forbids forcing the 80,000-triangle guidance if reduction harms the planes.

Residual risk to judge in the final captures: the wide lower footprint and 1.02 outline margin could still alter the visible lower rim. Reject a mushroom base, radial pedestal, rounded front mass, or lost terrace breaks. The voxel remesh is a global reconstruction candidate, not proof of preserved exterior identity.
