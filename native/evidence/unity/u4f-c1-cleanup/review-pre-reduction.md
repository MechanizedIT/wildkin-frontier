# U4F-C1 pre-reduction gate

**PRE_REDUCTION_HOLD**

Independent review of the post-repair matched boards rejects this working solid as a pristine render source. Striped/moiré surface artifacts remain across the visible rock, the underside retains a dark radial/star pattern, and the raw-to-reference art-direction gap remains. Although the retained mesh passes closure topology checks and its silhouette closely follows the raw, it does not pass the visual gate. Reduction and stamp-source construction are not authorized for this candidate.
