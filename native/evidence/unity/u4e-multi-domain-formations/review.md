# Independent U4E review

**Final disposition: HOLD.** The independent reviewer inspected the generated evidence, actual Unity views and Player capture. Technical contact/edit/regeneration evidence is credible, but the formation-readability requirement is not met.

## First pass

The 20-seed gallery, metrics, contacts, edit/regeneration records, tests and Player build established strong technical evidence. The gallery and standalone Player image were framed too distantly for an adequate visual quality judgment, so the visual gate was held pending one bounded presentation change.

## Bounded remediation

The implementation adjusted only presentation: gallery tile spacing and camera scale, label placement, hero/alternate camera framing, Player camera framing and contact-line visibility. Final captures and Player build were regenerated. Formation geometry, recipe, placement/contact semantics and domain data were not changed.

## Final re-review

The framing problem is resolved enough to read the formations. The visual result is still **HOLD**: several upper/cap stones appear to float or remain visibly separated from their supports. Hero seeds 7004, 7017, 7010 and 7015 show the issue; weakest seed 7019 also retains it. In seed 7004 and the seed-7000 Player view, flank stones appear separated from the foundation. The measured contact graph is useful geometric evidence, but it does not remove the perceptual gap or establish believable interlocking/stacking.

The visual target therefore remains unmet after the single bounded remediation. Stop at U4E; do not start U5. No additional repair was recommended in this review.

## Gate summary

- **PASS:** deterministic accepted 20-seed technical gallery, four archetypes, stable child identities and independent 0.25/0.125 m domains.
- **PASS:** geometry-derived terrain/sibling contacts and bidirectional sampled-overlap diagnostics; the recorded domain pairs have no sampled solid overlap.
- **PASS:** one-child edit changes matter and mesh only for the edited domain, rebuilds bounded regions, preserves terrain/sibling hashes and refreshes incident contact pairs.
- **PASS:** pristine recipe-only regeneration reproduces formation, child, source, mesh and contact-graph hashes with a compact descriptor.
- **PASS:** focused/full EditMode, PlayMode, and standalone Windows Player build/runtime execution.
- **HOLD:** visual coherence and believable interlocking remain insufficient in several formations after the framing repair.

U4C3's documented scope boundary is retained: arbitrary trilinear interior connectivity and higher-genus surfaces are not qualified. This review does not establish production-scale domain storage/performance, collision/support/collapse, dynamic transfer or a production architecture decision.
