# Sunscar weathered rib V1 — independent Gate A massing review

**Decision: HOLD — 3.8/10 target likeness.** The candidate is a clean, grounded, mobile-scale closed prop: actual bounds match 4.4 × 2.1 × 1.6 m, the audit reports one component, no non-two-face edges, no non-adjacent overlaps, and 140 triangles. Its 48/96 px silhouette remains visible. Those are construction facts, not visual admission.

Against the approved reference, the massing reads as a long roof wedge. The left side has a modest rise, but it does not form the reference's distinct raised shoulder and layered broken end. The cap is one nearly uninterrupted ramp, while the dark triangular centre-fan recesses read as repeated dimples/slots rather than the broad, shallow stratified sandstone ledges. Front/rear views expose generic faceted prism ends instead of asymmetric weathered volume. The disclosed zero-volume cap-adjacent BVH contact is not currently a visible defect, but the repair audit must classify or eliminate it instead of treating it as a clean self-intersection pass.

## One focused R2 repair

Keep the declared envelope, single opaque material, and one closed grounded mesh, but replace the centre-fan detail method with **two genuinely connected stepped cross-section bands**: a high, broad left shoulder/upper shelf over the first third; then a lower continuous cap shelf across the middle/right. Each band needs a real bounded perimeter loop and a vertical/angled face transition shared with the shell, so it changes the outer silhouette in the source three-quarter and side views. Shape the left end as the shoulder's blunt broken continuation and taper the right into the lower cap, rather than leaving both as flat prism caps. Use sparse planar erosion cuts only after those major bands exist. Re-run component, two-face, grounding, bounds, normals, and self-contact audits before render, and explain the remaining cap-adjacent contact if it persists. Do not spend the repair on more inward centre fans, colour variation, or extra noise.
