# Sunscar weathered rib V1 — independent reference review

**Decision: PASS as a prospective construction reference; HOLD on dimensions and placement until a measured plan.** The actual image contains one complete, opaque, grounded sandstone mass on a clean background. Its silhouette is strongly wider than tall, with a connected low base, blunt right end, raised asymmetric left shoulder, long sloping top break, and readable shallow bedding/erosion planes. It does not contaminate the subject with crystals, vegetation, UI, architectural gaps, or a vertical monolith. Its warm faceted material and restrained texture suit the selected Sunscar destination direction.

The reference is sufficient to guide a single mobile prop, but it is perspective art rather than recovered geometry. Hidden-side volume, exact ledge depths, contact footprint, collision shape, and placement remain unknown. The construction plan must turn the visible long continuous mass into one coherent closed mesh; it should avoid thin stacked slabs, a cave/arch void, or a tall cliff reading.

## Required scale correction before plan approval

The declaration's proposed envelope is **4.40 m × 2.10 m × 1.60 m**. Its stated feasibility band is **3.8–4.8 m wide, 1.3–1.9 m deep, 1.8–2.5 m high**. Thus both **depth (2.10 m)** and **height (1.60 m)** fall outside that old band; the declaration incorrectly says only height is exceptional. This target does not establish either dimension. A measured planning step may choose a coherent reconciled envelope based on the actual support and portrait projection, then state it as a proposal rather than an image-derived fact.

No Blender build, export, collider, or runtime placement is admitted by this reference PASS. The existing kit audit and full-geometry projection remain required before deciding whether this bounded asset is built or used.
