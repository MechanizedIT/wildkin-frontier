## Remaining visual work, not blockers for this bounded slice

1. Give the new yard a more deliberate clearing edge: a few low shrubs, scuffed soil and perimeter transition clusters, while preserving the useful central building space. Current broad smooth green ground and sparse isolated stones are substantially thinner than the target's organic yard frame.
2. Tune the berm-facing panel heights or terrain meeting points in the northeast/east corners so the top rail remains deliberate and similar lengths of wall remain exposed. Current ground contact is sound, but the stepped burial lacks the target's clean installation rhythm.
3. Reduce the fine, repeated stipple visible on large ivory panel faces and add restrained broad facet/value variation. This would better match the target's quiet matte surface at mobile scale.
4. If refining portrait interaction later, evaluate a subtle edge cue for a nearby offscreen console. Current direct approach is usable, but changing orientation near its reach boundary can hide the action until the console re-enters view.

Existing pod, services and the wider prototype environment were explicitly outside replacement/model-match scope. No old saved Author asset-catalog merge fixture was run. No full suite, packaged/offline launch, sustained FPS benchmark, earned fresh journey or physical phone was repeated by this reviewer. No production files or commits changed.
