# Ironspine narrow rockbank — independent construction-target review V2

## Decision: PASS as a bounded visual construction reference

V2 corrects the V1 sheet's conflicting proportions. Its labeled orthographic elevations now coherently describe the authoritative modeling envelope: 3.6 m local X length, 1.0 m local Z depth, and 2.0 m height. Treat those declared dimensions as the measurable constraints; the sheet itself remains an illustrative construction reference, not a source of recovered physical measurements. The separate placement envelope is only conservative support/footprint evidence and does not prove a finished mesh, visible fit, collision, or runtime admission.

The reference supplies a feasible single-subject direction for one narrow, opaque, grounded bank: a low, elongated continuous stone mass with closed ends, a gently asymmetric crown, and several broad strata. It is suitable for a low-poly modeled asset under the stated envelope.

There is still image ambiguity: repeated horizontal shelf breaks and dense fracture lines can read as stacked masonry or a bench stair from some views. The builder must therefore use one connected, irregular rock hull with only three or four *uneven, eroded* ledges, varied break positions and continuous transition faces. It must avoid separately piled blocks, regular courses, or a literal stepped retaining wall. The source plan must independently prove a closed connected mesh, declared bounds, opaque material, grounded contact, and narrow placement support before any runtime work.

V1 remains HOLD as historical evidence; this verdict applies only to `ironspine-narrow-rockbank-construction-target-v2.png`.
