# Ironspine narrow rockbank — independent R1 massing review

## Decision: HOLD — 3.4 / 10 visual fit

The normal-only correction is technically appropriate and must remain documented: the initial cap winding was inward even though the partial/global signed-volume calculation appeared positive. The corrected render evidence reports directed-edge consistency, outward caps, opaque cap coverage, one component, and the intended game-axis bounds. That satisfies a topology precondition; it does not validate the original proof's former `positiveOutwardWinding` claim or the visual target.

R1 misses the defining rockbank identity. In side, end, and three-quarter views it is a tall, smooth-faced stepped retaining wall: three straight continuous horizontal ledges, flat planar bands, vertical cut ends, and a roof-like crown. It has almost none of the target's broken uneven ledges, irregular rock transitions, blunt eroded ends, or faceted volume. A material/palette pass cannot repair this silhouette.

## One focused R2 repair

Replace the seven repeated stair-profile rings with one connected irregular rock hull whose three broad ledges are **interrupted** at different X positions and joined by diagonal/sloped transition faces. Break the strict horizontal shelf lines, vary front/rear footprint and crest depth across stations, blunt both ends, and add only enough asymmetric faceting to read as eroded stone at game scale. Preserve the 3.6 × 2.0 × 1.0 m envelope, one opaque material, one connected closed volume, the corrected game→Blender→glTF mapping, and separate concave-cap auditing. Require a changed literal input/source review and untextured side/end/three-quarter massing before any material or native projection work.

This consumes the initial structural candidate; the cap-normal correction does not add a shape pass.
