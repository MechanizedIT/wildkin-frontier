# Ironspine narrow rockbank — independent R2 massing review

## Decision: HOLD — 4.2 / 10 visual fit

R2 is numerically and topologically cleaner than R1: its literal input remains intact, the pre-render directed-edge/cap/opacity checks pass without a repair, and the crest/end vary more than R1. The attempted shelf interruptions are visible as small notches in the source three-quarter view.

They do not change the principal read. The side remains a tall striped wall with two nearly continuous narrow ledge lines, a steep roof wedge, and a vertical terminal mass. The end still reads as a stepped retaining-wall section. R2 therefore improves the failed construction evidence without reaching the V2 target's low, broad, irregular continuous rockbank. A material pass cannot correct that silhouette.

## One final R3 repair, only if a new plan passes review

Use a different massing method: replace the repeated upright nine-point stair profile with a **low, broad asymmetric boulder-bank hull**. Reduce the visible shoulder height substantially within the conservative `3.6 × 1.0 × 2.0 m` envelope (the declared maximum is not an obligation), consume more of the existing depth for a rounded/bulged cross-section, and form three broad oblique rock planes that merge into the body rather than narrow horizontal shelf bands. Keep only a shallow left shoulder and a much lower, blunt right runout; break the side contour with irregular diagonal facets, not vertical wall panels. Construct it as one closed connected opaque volume with individually audited end caps.

The R3 plan must show its proposed actual reduced height/depth and source-facing versus side silhouette before execution. Do not relabel another profile-tweak, palette, or fracture mask as this structural repair. If a feasible changed hull cannot be demonstrated, close the asset visit HOLD rather than consume the last pass.
