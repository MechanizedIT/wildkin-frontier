# Independent visual review — Lantern log manual R3

**Decision: HOLD for art and runtime admission.** This is a useful, valid manual-model checkpoint, not a target-quality lantern log yet.

I inspected all seven 512px views and the principal 96px/48px renders against `reference-v2.png`, rather than inferring success from the audit. The six-cap hierarchy reads at 96px and remains recognizable at 48px; the hero cap, three medium caps, and young caps form a clear colony. The build also retains a visible shallow near-end recess and a cohesive single-prop silhouette. Those are useful gains.

The reference scores approximately **8.2/10** as a construction direction. This candidate scores **5.7/10** visually: colony grouping 7.0, cap readability 7.0, log silhouette/material read 4.3, end/recess read 4.8, and target likeness 5.0. Its long, nearly uniform 12-sided shell reads as a manufactured pale rail. Six narrow parallel bark strips reinforce that read. The near recess is geometrically closed but presents as a regular socket rather than the shallow, broken, weathered end in the reference. The reference's broad broken grain planes, moss-integrated irregularity, tapered bulk, and grounded weight are absent.

The washed clay/grey palette and dark studio background are presentation issues; a controlled sRGB/neutral-lighting correction could improve cap and wood separation. They do not explain the main shortfall. The structural silhouette remains too straight, smooth, and evenly banded in every view, including the small renders.

The numeric evidence is accepted for its limited purpose: the corrected input/builder hashes are recorded; 22 closed components and 1,452 triangles passed the component audit; the log records 16 actual ground-plane triangles with `.3416988 m²` area; local cap-underside ray evidence and bounds-fit render margins are present. This does not establish mesh union, runtime readiness, collision, placement, harvesting, or visual admission.

## One focused repair, if a further candidate remains authorized

Replace only the log body's uniform rail treatment with a low-poly, tapered, irregular woody mass: preserve all six current cap/stem centerlines and their successful attachment gates, but use a small number of deliberately uneven cross-sections and broad broken longitudinal planes, with a thicker interrupted near end around the shallow recess. Make bark read as 3–4 integrated broad fissure planes rather than six raised parallel strips. Re-run the same grounding/contact/framing/audit gates before rendered comparison. A palette-only pass would not close the held target gap.

No runtime admission follows from retaining this evidence. The original images under `C:\art\source\lantern-log-v1\manual-r3\candidate-r3\renders` and their repository copies are visually consistent for this review.
