# Rootbound buttress study — Trial B initial massing review

**Scope:** initial untextured Gate A review. This is neither a final visual decision nor an export, collider, runtime, or placement approval.

**Decision: HOLD.** The executed trial demonstrates portions of the numerical skeleton, including a visibly returning leader path and non-chord branch directions, but it does not yet translate those controls into the approved target shape.

The actual all-angle frames show three independent failures:

1. **The wood is assembled, not grown.** Major branches and roots visibly overlap the trunk rather than sharing bridged junction faces. The five long, capped root prongs make a radial fan with open valleys instead of the target's thick continuous buttress base.
2. **The root geometry has no contoured web/collar.** Without shaped shared lower surfaces, the source-facing tree looks elevated on separated legs and the side/rear views cannot establish a closed planted mass.
3. **The canopy ignores the common-plan canopy requirement.** Three simple icosphere-like volumes substitute for the required two-to-four deep irregular hulls per left/high/right group. They do not create the source's layered foliage, lower lobes, or supported windows.

## One consolidated repair after the section-table junction clarification is frozen

Preserve the listed skeleton control-point paths, computed bends, and taper targets. Build a manifold parent/child junction patch at T3 and T4 using disjoint parent-ring arcs, then bridge the five root trajectories into a continuous contoured T0/T1 collar. Use the geometry budget for rounded buttress webs, shallow closed valleys, and irregular canopy hull clusters—not a noisy global union or broader root fan. Each canopy group needs multiple deep, connected/interlocking faceted hulls seated over an actual branch stub, with clear gaps between left/high/right groups. Re-render all five untextured views after that one repair; do not proceed to palette/export from this massing.
