# Rootbound tree modeling workflow comparison

Fresh owner-authorized study, September 14, 2026. The previous three tree candidates remain held and untouched. This study starts from the same independently reviewed `../rootbound-buttress-v1/reference/target-v1.png`, with no prior tree geometry reused.

A separate modeling director applies the project-local `asset-modeling-director` skill and produces a measured construction brief. Separate builders run two fresh approaches sequentially; an independent reviewer judges the plan, untextured massing and final game-sized model.

- Trial A: applicable arjun988/blender-skills reference matching, Blender modeling and vegetation guidance.
- Trial B: applicable RobLe3/cc-blender-skill reference reconstruction, contour/landmark fitting and volumetric modeling guidance.
- Each approach: one initial candidate and up to two focused repairs, stopping early on PASS. Intermediate views do not reset the allowance. Same reference, 5–6m total height, ≤7m lateral extent, ≤5,000 triangles and one opaque material.
- Original sources and exact pinned commits/hashes are in `community-source-manifest.json`. Markdown/license files were downloaded into the ignored `.dream-loop/rootbound-modeling-study/vendor/` folder. No global skill installation, remote code execution, package setup script or paid generation is involved.
- Live Blender 4.5.3 workbench is isolated from the user's scene. One builder/GPU job at a time. Reference authors, builder and independent judge remain separate.
- Perspective source measurements are approximate; hidden depth is a declared design inference. Flat source-textured cutouts do not satisfy this tree's all-angle volume requirement. No claim of pixel-exact reconstruction or a controlled general benchmark.
- Habitat runtime remains frozen. This study is nonshipping until separately documented admission and actual runtime proof. No push.

**Final study status:** Trial A completed three passes and remains **HOLD 7.4/10**. Its early massing improved markedly over the old primitive attempt, as Chris observed, but the target's bent trunk and layered canopy were not preserved strongly enough. Trial B completed three passes and remains **Gate A HOLD 1.0/10**, with 46 disconnected wood components and no palette/GLB/runtime admission. During the study Chris explicitly requested stronger target-derived curvature and section-by-section dimensions, bend angles and taper. The director added concrete proposed path/section tables before Trial B geometry. This was an intentional workflow refinement: B received more detailed guidance than A. Any difference in results therefore cannot be attributed solely to the two community skill packs. Both used the same original target, runtime envelope and bounded pass allowance.

The later section and junction revisions, technical review failures, executed departures and invalid object-count assumption are preserved explicitly. The workflow now requires applicable modeling guidance, independent technical approval of material plan revisions, frozen machine-readable inputs, retained executed source, and actual topology/visual evidence. More detailed plans did not guarantee correct construction.

Chris subsequently activated a continuous all-habitat goal with parallel Wildkin work and ongoing workflow refinement. This study remains closed within that goal; its failures do not authorize an immediate fourth tree pass. See `../../../docs/CONTINUOUS_HABITAT_ROTATION.md` for the live queue.
