# Rootbound buttress study — independent construction-plan review

**Reviewed inputs:** `construction-plan.md`, `reference-manifest.json`, the original `../../rootbound-buttress-v1/reference/target-v1.png`, and its approved construction-reference contract.

## Decision: HOLD pending three bounded plan corrections

The plan has the right overall method for a fresh volumetric trial: it separates visible observations from hidden-side inference, forbids old candidate reuse and primitive final forms, specifies a closed buttress/trunk/fork hierarchy, requires untextured all-angle massing before export, and retains the 5–6 m, <=7 m, <=5,000-triangle, one-opaque-material contract. Its proposed connected-loop/bridged-strip operations are sufficient for a faithful manual build once the following measurement errors are corrected. No builder should start from this version because two requirements would reward a narrow or artificially fanned silhouette inconsistent with the target.

## Required corrections

1. **Correct the source-facing height-to-width reading.** The target's complete visible silhouette is roughly as wide as it is tall (about 0.9 of image width versus about 0.9 of image height, allowing for perspective and irregular roots), not 1.7:1. The plan's derived `3.3–4.2 m` source-facing width for a 5.6 m model is therefore unsupported and too narrow for the reference's broad crown/root silhouette. Replace it with an explicitly approximate **source-facing visible spread around 4.8–5.8 m at a 5.6 m height**, with deeper unseen mass remaining an inference inside the <=7 m footprint. Treat exact front/depth relation as an all-angle massing judgment rather than recovered perspective fact.
2. **Make the fork-window gate screen-measurable.** The plan correctly says the source perspective camera is unknown, then requires a `0.70 m × 0.85 m` window “when projected from the source-facing camera.” That physical projected size cannot be verified without an invented camera calibration. Replace it with the already observed normalized target envelope (`x≈0.38–0.65`, `y≈0.39–0.58`) under a declared matching review camera/framing. The builder may choose plausible depth, but the review compares the resulting screen-space opening, branch support, and side volume.
3. **Do not require every toe to be visible in the one source-facing view.** The target view has occlusion at the root base. “Show all five root toes” in its three-quarter proof can force the starburst/fanning error seen in the held candidates. Require the source-facing render to show a broad continuous grounded base and several readable toe termini, then require rotating front/rear/left/right/three-quarter massing renders to establish all five distinct closed buttresses without tunnels.

## What remains binding after correction

The fresh-source rule, volumetric connected root and fork operations, 0.24 m-or-greater useful root thickness, three separate deep canopy groups, visible branch windows, neutral massing gate, exact export/game-scale gate, and separate reviewer remain appropriate. The plan must retain the distinction between observed screen-space landmarks and provisional rear/depth topology; its existing `reference-manifest.json` does this well.

After these text-level corrections, this common plan is fit for both sequential fresh builder trials. It does not itself admit a mesh, collider, runtime placement, or any reuse of held v1–v3 geometry.
