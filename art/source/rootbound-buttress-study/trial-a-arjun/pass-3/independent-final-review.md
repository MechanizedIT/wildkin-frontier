# Rootbound buttress study — Trial A pass-3 final review

**Scope:** final independent visual/model review. It does not admit a collider, runtime placement, protected-clearance change, or shipping asset.

**Reviewed evidence:** `final-renders/{front,rear,left,right,source_three_quarter,game_48,game_96}.png`, `rootbound-buttress-trial-a-repair.glb`, `glb-check.json`, the approved original reference, and the corrected common plan.

## Decision: HOLD — 7.4 / 10

Pass 3 improves the canopy over pass 2: its silhouette has more unequal downward fragments and less uniform horizontal banding, while the broad rooted base remains clear and grounded. The exact GLB is structurally lean and valid for its stated prop checks: SHA-256 `08a17ed82f79320f6c8bf919675ff53412a81e254a6bc15c29d12db080865b06`, 4,542 triangles, one material, no textures. The tree remains readable at 48 px and 96 px.

It still does not meet the 8/10 likeness threshold. The original target has a visibly bending, offset main shaft and substantial irregular fork geometry that carries a dense, organic, three-part canopy. Trial A remains dominated by a bulky near-straight taper and three broad, shallow geometric crowns. The final palette improves planes but cannot turn the canopy into the reference's layered foliage mass or give the trunk its missing directional gesture. It is a usable stylized tree candidate, not the target-specific Rootbound buttress anchor.

## Method-enforcement gap

The common plan identified a rightward S-lean, offset loop centers, shallow limb elbows, and normalized source-facing landmarks. The early Gate A decision correctly rejected thin/disconnected geometry but did not enforce those target-specific bend landmarks strongly enough; it accepted “plausible continuous forks” without a front-view trunk-path or limb-gesture comparison. That allowed a bulky near-straight shaft to survive into final export. Future modeling-director plans should make the source-facing trunk centerline and each major limb's bend a separate measurable/overlayable Gate A check before volume completion is passed. This is an enforcement lesson, not a reason to retroactively pass the model.

## Final status

Trial A’s three-pass allowance is exhausted. Retain its fresh source, exact GLB, all-angle evidence, and the held decision as useful study output. Do not integrate this GLB or infer any runtime/collider admission from the isolated nonshipping preview. A future separately authorized attempt should begin from the method-enforcement correction above, not a fourth Trial A repair.
