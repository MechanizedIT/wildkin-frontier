# Rootbound buttress study — Trial A pass-2 final model review

**Scope:** final asset visual review against the approved target and common plan. It does not admit a collider, runtime placement, protected-clearance change, or game asset.

**Reviewed evidence:** `final-renders/{front,rear,left,right,source_three_quarter,game_48,game_96}.png`, the final Blend and GLB, `glb-check.json`, the approved original reference, and the common construction plan.

## Decision: HOLD — 7.1 / 10

Trial A is a substantial improvement over the held rootbound-buttress-v1 attempts. The actual final renders show a grounded, broad continuous root base, a warm coherent trunk, supported three-way branching, and a readable silhouette at 48 px and 96 px. The final GLB is fresh, one-material, texture-free, and structurally under the stated limits (4,542 triangles / 10,073 vertices).

It does not yet reach the 8/10 target-admission threshold. The reference's foliage is an irregular deep aggregation of many supported faceted masses. Trial A still reads as three tidy, horizontally banded umbrella disks: the left/right side views make their shallow equatorial bands conspicuous, and the palette reinforces those bands rather than describing foliage volume. The result is a credible stylized tree but not the specific rooted Wildwood anchor promised by the target.

## One consolidated final repair

Keep the passed root/trunk/fork geometry and overall bounds. Rework only the three canopy groups using existing geometry budget: reshape the outer and lower hull vertices into uneven faceted clusters with staggered downward lobes, unequal top contours, and genuine oblique side planes; remove the continuous horizontal equator/band silhouette from every group. Reassign the single-material palette by structural plane—darker undersides/interiors, moss midplanes, restrained yellow-green upward facets—so color follows the newly volumetric lobe shapes instead of forming three flat stripes. Preserve the branch window and visible attachment stubs from all angles. Do not add leaf cards, textures, another material, canopy-wide scale, or more geometry beyond the 5,000-triangle cap.

Re-render the same seven final views after this one focused repair. If the canopy still reads as regular disks rather than three irregular supported clusters, retain Trial A as HOLD; do not extend its candidate loop.
