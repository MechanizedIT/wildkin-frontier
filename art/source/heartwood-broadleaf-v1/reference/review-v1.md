# Heartwood broadleaf reference v1 — independent input review

**Decision: PASS as a local single-image construction reference; no model admission.**

Source: `target-v1.png`, SHA-256 `0ad7b81d99f99cf70851ec3fde2101fb258b7d6bc8a102cf7dea25ce03e13b13`.

The image clearly supplies the missing Heartwood role: one compact, warm-trunked broadleaf perimeter canopy with a deep, irregular rounded crown. It is isolated against whitespace, grounded, and readable as a small mobile prop. The proposed 4.5–5.5 m height, 3.5–5 m width, at most 5,000 triangles, and one opaque material are plausible only if the foliage is built as a small number of faceted clustered volumes rather than literal leaf cards or individually modeled leaves.

## Likeness gates for a future candidate

- Keep the foreground read: one flared, grounded trunk, visible root spokes, then a clear lower fork into left and right supporting limbs before the crown.
- Build a layered, asymmetric deep crown with separated silhouette lobes and interior shadow gaps; a single sphere, flat cap, or thin leaf spray fails the target role.
- Keep joins continuous at root-to-trunk and trunk-to-forks. The root base must sit on ground and the crown must visually attach to the supporting branches.
- Judge at intended game size as well as source view. The wide crown and warm fork need remain distinct from the existing pink heartwood tree and sparse redwood.

## Hidden-depth limits

This one view does not prove rear root count, backside branching, crown depth, or collision footprint. A builder must make restrained, coherent backside choices within the stated envelope and expose them in neutral front/rear/side/three-quarter renders. It must not invent a grove, landmark-scale tree, extra fauna/flora, or a new runtime behavior to compensate for those unknowns.
