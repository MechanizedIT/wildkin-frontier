# Explorer v9 motion review — PASS

Reviewed the eleven v9 pose renders and the supplied complete Sneak, Walk, and Run cycle videos from front, side, and three-quarter views, alongside actual-game desktop/phone captures. Evidence is the exported GLB with SHA-256 `7900868f831c723b60edb15b8321d4818754223db11ef80e6766c91b9b214104`.

The locomotion poses read as distinct crouched sneak, light jog/walk, and faster run. The brief flight in Walk and Run is consistent with their energetic styles; Sneak remains the lower, more grounded gait. The visible step exchange is coherent, with no obvious foot inversion, limb collapse, knee reversal, torso tear, or backpack separation. The supplied gait measurements also pair the foot travel to their stated controller speeds; counts alone were not used as a visual basis for this pass.

Jump, Fall, Dodge, Climb, Mantle, Attack, and Hurt each hold a readable body intent. Arms move from the shoulders without a visible shoulder pinch; elbows and wrists stay connected; the bent torso and hips remain intact in dodge/hurt; and the boots stay proportionate during the airborne poses. The actual-game attack capture further confirms that animation does not break the held-tool hand fit.

Minor limitation: action poses are restrained, and close review exposes the same coarse glove/cuff texture transitions noted in the model review. At the supplied game camera scale, neither produces a visible deformation failure or unclear action read.

This is a motion visual pass only; runtime adoption remains the parent’s decision.
