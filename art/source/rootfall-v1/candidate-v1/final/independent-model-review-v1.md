# Rootfall V1 final — independent model gate

Reviewer: `camp_native_review`, independent of the model author. Reviewed all nine full fresh-GLB PNGs in `.dream-loop/overnight2-rootfall/model-v1/final/`, the frozen manifest/README, the approved Explorer master, and CLOSED V3 plus `layout.md`. No browser, Blender, production changes or test run. This review concerns this frozen `final/` candidate, not the earlier diagnostic studies.

## Verdict

**HOLD — 7.2/10.** Composition/readability 2.2/3, lighting 2.6/3, materials/biological identity 2.0/3, details 0.4/1. The model communicates a fallen obstruction and its removal, but the manufactured-looking cuts, regular trunk and unconvincing fiber attachment leave it below the independent 8.0 model threshold. Technical economy and effort do not compensate for these visible defects.

This is a neutral/model-camera gate only. It neither rejects nor certifies actual ridge grounding, live harvesting, collision, approach/return traversal, interaction UI or phone performance. The accepted CLOSED target remains unchanged; no generated OPEN image is required.

## Required corrections

1. **Make the two working sites read as damaged wood.** `notch-detail.png` shows a rectangular tan recess with three near-rectangular brown plugs. The straight sill and square sides read as a small fitted socket or panel. In `game-scale-closed.png`, the two sites become tiny identical fittings rather than legible shallow fractures. Keep the fixed cut planes, roughly 0.3m vertical limit and intact bark/core above, below and behind. Replace the rectangular aperture with an irregular wedge or chipped fracture contour; vary the three removable chips so their exposed edges join that fracture naturally. Use restrained sapwood contrast and directional torn edges to make the working sites readable at the same camera. Do not restore full pale rings or enlarge them into through-cuts.

2. **Restore the target's continuous, asymmetric biological form.** `front-closed.png` and `quarter-closed.png` show a long, almost level cylinder divided into regular longitudinal stations, with repeated horizontal color belts. Angular root fingers attach at its ends and sparse teal plates sit like separate flat patches. The target has a more integrated broad left buttress, a tapering right branch system and broken, uneven bark/moss masses. Preserve the envelope, fixed planes and common state geometry, but vary the major trunk cross-sections/taper and blend the buttress/branch transitions into the body. Break the visible regular stripe rhythm with larger irregular bark facets and connected, restrained teal growth masses. A few bounded leaf clusters can help match the target, but dressing alone will not fix the cylindrical body. Broad form changes matter more than adding triangles or surface noise.

3. **Place the two lashings around the supported timber joints.** `brace-contact.png` shows three tan arcs sitting on the teal bark patch above the place where the timber tops converge. They do not visibly bind the timbers. The poles themselves appear to meet the retained body and their feet sit on the neutral ground, so the issue is the attachment depiction, not a claim that the entire support floats. Move/shape each lashing around the actual upper members or a clearly seated common joint. Show the continuous wrap and a small credible tie/return from the same close angle. Keep four recognizable timbers, two lashings and the clear lane.

## What passes in the visible evidence

- The CLOSED body occupies the ground-level route without an obvious walk-under gap. The two notches are partial front damage, not complete circumferential severing; retaining this continuity is correct.
- Front and quarter CLOSED/OPEN pairs retain the same visible roots, bank rocks, branch silhouette and teal marks. The central interval is removed without the earlier generated-pair problem of changing the surviving tree. The manifest fixes planes at X=-1.65/+1.65; pictures support the intended transition, while exact topology/plane measurements remain manifest/validator evidence.
- OPEN has an unmistakable broad lane. Braces stay on the two flanks, with two distinct timbers per side and feet visibly supported in the neutral setup. Nothing in these staged views obviously crosses the central corridor.
- Matte brown, muted teal and tan belong to the Explorer's palette and broad faceted rendering family. There is no glossy metal, noisy photoreal texture, accidental extra organism or unexplained floating major form.
- The Explorer scale views establish a substantial obstacle and a comfortable-looking opening without requiring a changed avatar or closer camera. The high outer tip is cropped by the fixture; it is not silently credited as a full native silhouette check.
- Reported economy is modest: six GLBs total 169,260 bytes /2,198 unique triangles /8 unique draws; scene instances differ by state. These are manifest/author metrics, not a native performance measurement.
