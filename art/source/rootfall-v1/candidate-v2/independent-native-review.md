# Rootfall V2 — independent native visual gate

**Verdict: HOLD — 7.4/10 for native presentation.** The actual game-camera sequence has a substantial, readable Rootfall event and the repaired passage works visually: paired braces are clear, the central route is broad, and the return view makes the opened connection understandable. It is not ready for native visual admission because the CLOSED form and first-cut state do not make the work sites legible enough at normal landscape-camera scale. The two intended shallow cuts read as tiny gold/cream fittings in a deep dark underside, while the body reads partly as an elevated arch/bridge rather than an unmistakably ground-blocking fallen root.

This review is independent of the neutral-model score and reviews the supplied native captures only. It does not claim a fresh earned expedition, a physical-phone test, measured performance, or a full campaign playthrough.

- Native captures: `.dream-loop/overnight2-rootfall/native-closed.png`, `native-first-cut.png`, `native-brace-ready.png`, `native-open.png`, and `native-return-exit.png` at the actual 844x390 landscape HUD/camera framing.

It then uses actual keyboard movement/collision, production auto-harvest to perform each cut, world UI clicks for Brace/Travel, reciprocal return walking, and literal Continue reload. It records no direct save-flag or geometry mutation. That is good bounded proof of this event route, but it is not an earned campaign journey.

## Native findings

### What passes

- **Scale and state contrast:** CLOSED occupies the native approach as a genuine landmark rather than a small prop. OPEN makes a recognizably different event state without swapping in a new unrelated tree; the separated retained ends and braces explain the cleared middle.
- **Open passage lane:** In `native-open.png` the player has an obvious, central route through the gap. The far beacon/exit remains visible beyond it. `native-return-exit.png` confirms that the same opening remains readable from the reciprocal side. The diagnostic also records actual walk-through to z −32.78, Travel to section 2, a return at z −31.5, and a reloaded repaired state.
- **Braces and contextual action:** Both timber pairs are visible against the grass, feet appear planted, and their lashings make a legible support story. In `native-brace-ready.png`, the contextual **Brace passage** button has a good reachable placement and explicit material cost. The brief completion toast in `native-open.png` is acceptable transient HUD; these captures precede the separate Jump UI work.
- **Style and HUD:** The brown/teal faceted treatment belongs reasonably with the live world and Explorer. The native HUD leaves the root, work action, player, and lane readable; no zero-HUD requirement is appropriate here.

### Blocking native-readability issues

1. **The cut sites do not read as cut sites at play scale.** In CLOSED and first-cut they are two minute light marks buried in the nearly black lower trunk shadow. They can be mistaken for metallic fixtures or texture glints. `native-first-cut.png` communicates “1/2 roots cut” through its message, but the picture alone does not show which section has been worked or why the player should seek the second site. A player should be able to recognize shallow exposed-bark chips from the ordinary approach without relying on diagnostic auto-harvest or the toast.

2. **CLOSED is visually ambiguous as an obstruction.** The long horizontal underside is very dark and appears to hang between widely planted root fingers. The collision diagnostic proves it blocks, but the picture invites a question of whether the Explorer should fit underneath it. It needs a stronger ground-blocking read in the central travel lane while preserving the intended fallen-tree silhouette. This is a visual correction, not a request for an invisible collision wall or a larger replacement model.

3. **First-cut visual progression is too subtle.** The removed west section is not promptly distinguishable in the native frame: the whole root still reads nearly identical until the brace state. The state message is useful supporting feedback, but the living geometry needs a clearly visible missing/chipped work-side result before the second cut, so the two-cut requirement feels physical.

## Focused correction brief

Keep the V2 overall silhouette, fixed cut planes, retained roots and brace positions. Do not create a new OPEN tree, add a course, or enlarge the whole event.

- Turn each near-facing target into a small but player-legible irregular pale sapwood chip with a dark rim and intact bark above/below. Aim it toward the approach camera and raise/brighten it only enough to survive the trunk's underside shadow; retain the shallow, non-circumferential cut contract.
- Make the first removed seam expose a distinct broken face/negative notch visible from the approach. It should identify the completed side at a glance while the remaining side still visibly blocks the lane.
- Add or reshape only local lower bark/root mass at the central CLOSED lane so the form visibly meets or nearly meets the travel surface. Preserve credible outside root fingers and do not use a hidden wall. Check the same native angle afterwards to ensure the opening remains a deliberate 3m-class gap once braced.
- Recheck the five supplied states at 844x390, including player-scale cut targeting and the brace-button overlap. The existing diagnostic walk/travel/return/reload proof can be reused only if the changed render/collision shape still supports it.

## Evidence boundary

The harness demonstrates this exact diagnostic sequence: supported Rootfall start, blocked approach, two production harvest completions, brace interaction, opened walking passage, outward Travel, reciprocal return, and reload persistence. It does not establish a newly earned full route, touch controls, Jump UI behavior, physical-phone legibility, overall FPS/load cost, or acceptance by the owner. A focused corrected native capture can close this art gate; the broader gameplay and phone gates remain separate.
