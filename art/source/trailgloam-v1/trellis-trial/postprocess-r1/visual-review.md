# Independent textured derivative review

**RETAIN AS A USEFUL TRELLIS DERIVATIVE; runtime/admission HOLD.** The actual postprocess GLB (`e223fdd1…c1933`) gives Trailgloam a strong, readable low-poly silhouette: a broad teal shell, bright paired amber fronds, a compact face with warm eyes, and **eight** grounded articulated legs (four per side). The matched textured and neutral views retain the raw master’s useful organic mass while making its limbs, cuffs, and feet more legible. At 96 px the amber pair and body/leg separation still read; at 48 px it reduces to a recognizable dark-bodied, bright-crested creature. The generated texture is restrained and serviceable rather than an over-detailed surface.

This is a practical gain over the raw untextured master and is worth preserving as the current TRELLIS candidate. It does not need to exactly reproduce every reference facet to be useful. The main visual debt is that the feet remain very large, uniform dark blocks and the side/rear legs form an evenly spaced mechanical skirt. The shell is a little smooth and low relative to the target’s more strongly faceted, layered body. These are later anatomy/material decisions, not reasons to discard the derivative.

The next asset path is anatomy-and-rig preparation, not runtime placement: fit the actual eight chains and paired frond/head anchors from measured mesh landmarks, preserve the 1K generated texture where viable, then inspect ground contacts and motion. Eight legs are a retained actual-model difference; the earlier six-leg plan was an agent proposal, not an owner-locked requirement. The inspector reports 1,048 **UV-split vertex-connected** components, which must not be described as 1,048 detached physical solids. Its position-welded diagnostic instead finds 80 components with zero boundary edges and 26 nonmanifold edges. These are flags for focused anatomy, weighting and deformation checks; they do not prove that every region must be reconstructed or that the rendered candidate cannot be rigged. Collision can remain a separately reviewed proxy.

The export itself is a visually successful derivative, not evidence of runtime behavior, collision, scale, animation, save ownership, or species admission. The native postprocess completed, but `gpu_samples` is empty even though logs show all native phases; peak/phase guard execution is therefore unverified and should be corrected in a future tool revision without rerunning this candidate merely for telemetry.
