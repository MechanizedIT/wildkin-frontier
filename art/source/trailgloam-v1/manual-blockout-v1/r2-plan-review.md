## Required corrections before the Blender run

1. **Resolve every mismatched loop count.** A six-vertex cuff cannot be directly bridged to a five-sided upper prism, and a five-sided lower cannot directly bridge a four-sided hoof. Specify either all six-sided limb/ankle/hoof attachment loops, or named closed transition collars (six→five at cuff/upper and five→four at lower/hoof) with a declared triangulation pattern and no internal cap. “Matching six-vertex cuff/upper loops” contradicts the stated five-sided upper.
2. **Make the head a connected shell feature.** Cut a named forward shell boundary loop and bridge the five-face wedge's neck loop into it, then remove mutually interior faces. A 0.10 m overlapping wedge alone repeats the disconnected/interpenetrating construction the amendment is meant to replace.
3. **Make frond sockets actual holes.** For each separate collar, cut a distinct posterior shell boundary loop and bridge it to the collar before joining the closed folded blade. Preserve the 0.10 m socket-edge gap. Do not merely overlap collars onto an uncut shell.

After those corrections, **GO for the single R2 Gate-A neutral massing repair**. It must retain the required one-component, normals, non-manifold, and triangle-BVH checks; the latter needs a recorded result that excludes only identical/edge-adjacent pairs.
