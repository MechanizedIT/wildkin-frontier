# Independent R5 method-probe visual review

**Evidence:** nine guarded Blender renders, array/proof parity receipt, and the six-view Trailgloam reference. This is one representative +X chain and one frond only; it is not a creature candidate or an admission decision.

## Method verdict: PASS for reusable articulated-chain construction

At 512px, the saucer body, three-section bent chain, cuffs, and grounded hoof read as distinct, substantial forms. The three-quarter view makes the coxa-to-hoof bend especially clear, and the underside does not reveal an open tube. At 96px the chain still reads as a bent appendage with a separate foot; at 48px it is understandably only a small articulated cue. This is sufficient evidence that closed individual solids, cuff-contained link ends, and the full lower cuff-ring-in-hoof contact method can be reused in a separately designed full candidate.

The receipt supports that construction conclusion: all 10 literal parts retained closed topology, the CPU containment results were replayed against the pinned arrays, and Blender proved float32 XYZ/index/loop-triangle parity. It does **not** prove a welded creature shell, six legs, a head, animation, or runtime behavior.

## Frond/identity verdict: redesign required before a full candidate

The dorsal collar dominates the silhouette as a dark rectangular stalk. The amber folded blade is visible mainly as a tiny tip, including at 512px, and disappears as a recognizable frond at 96/48px. The probe therefore does not yet carry Trailgloam's rooted amber-frond identity. It also provides no evidence for the required paired fronds or six visible grounded leg chains.

Before a full neutral Gate-A model, author a fresh layout that keeps this cuffed chain/hoof method but changes the dorsal assembly: two broad, outward-facing folded amber blades must emerge visibly from low integrated collars, with their body penetration and screen-space exposure measured in the planned front/three-quarter views. Plan all six chain roots and the head in the same source-of-truth geometry, then use full all-angle and 48/96px renders for creature admission. No further R5 probe rebuild is warranted.
