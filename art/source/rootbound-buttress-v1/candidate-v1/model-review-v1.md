## Decision: HOLD — 2.7 / 10

The candidate does not reproduce the approved reference's grounded buttress-trunk-and-three-lobe construction. Across the neutral views, a flat-topped tapered stump carries radial pointed struts from its midsection; those struts read as a detached starburst rather than roots meeting a trunk or branches growing from it. The foliage is a set of separate near-spherical balls, so the model loses the reference's coherent supported canopy lobes. At 48 px and 96 px, the result reads as a small spiky object with floating colored balls, not a usable rooted tree anchor.

The 740-triangle count is not a remedy for the visible structural failure. The recorded 6.82 m height also exceeds the approved 5–6 m range; its 6.15 m lateral width is within the permitted maximum.

## Three consequential gaps

1. **Grounded base fails.** The root-like pieces originate high on the trunk, leave conspicuous open gaps, and do not form three to five closed buttresses that visibly reach and merge with the ground base. The proposed lower-core box cannot make the visible unsupported tips honest.
2. **Branch structure fails.** A cut flat stump and rigid radial spikes replace the reference's continuous trunk-to-branch junctions. This is the principal silhouette and biological-construction loss in every neutral angle.
3. **Canopy identity and game-scale reading fail.** Disconnected spherical foliage blobs neither form the three irregular faceted lobes nor reveal purposeful branch windows. The game-size frames preserve the same toy/starburst reading rather than a Rootbound route-frame.

## One consolidated structural repair

Rebuild the asset's hierarchy instead of scaling, smoothing, or recoloring v1. Start with one 5.5–5.9 m faceted tapered trunk closed at ground level. Merge three to five broad, low buttress wedges continuously into its bottom 0.8–1.0 m, with each outer end planted on the ground and no traversable root gaps. Above the walking line, split the trunk into three visibly joined, unequal branches; attach one irregular porous faceted foliage lobe to each branch. Use a few large opaque planes/volumes per lobe, keeping readable voids around the branch forks, rather than balls or literal leaf cards. Keep the complete maximum footprint at or under 7 m, constrain solid collision to the genuinely continuous lower trunk/root core, and then re-render all six evidence views plus 48/96 px before review.
