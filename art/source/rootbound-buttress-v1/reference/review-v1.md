## Consequential mesh constraints

1. **Do not model the hundreds of depicted leaves literally.** Convert each of the three foliage lobes to a few connected, matte faceted masses with purposeful gaps; preserve the trunk and branch windows. Leaf-card clutter or a fused opaque canopy would erase the route-side framing at portrait scale and create unnecessary mobile cost.
2. **Keep the asset Rootbound-native.** The source reads somewhat like a familiar broadleaf oak. Retain its warm faceted buttress/trunk construction, but simplify foliage into the game's thick, restrained alien canopy language rather than introducing realistic leaf veins, a deciduous leaf carpet, or a generic Earth-tree material.
3. **Keep roots and collision honest.** Build three to five legible broad buttresses into a closed ground base; do not turn gaps between roots into walk-under arches. The collider must cover only the genuinely solid trunk/root mass and retain the existing protected route, forage, and wildlife clearances.
