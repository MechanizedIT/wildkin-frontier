# Survey Module V2 — independent neutral model review

Reviewer: `camp_native_review`, independent of production implementer. All six V2 screenshots were inspected at full supplied dimensions against the unchanged target and the retained V1 HOLD findings. All seven candidate GLB hashes were independently checked against disk and match the V2 manifest.

## Verdict

**PASS — exactly 8.0/10 for the bounded neutral-model gate.** Composition/form 2.3/3, lighting/palette 2.6/3, materials/surface treatment 2.5/3, details 0.6/1. This reaches the >=8 threshold; it does not admit the actual world placement, native-camera presentation, collision, phone performance or owner aesthetic acceptance.

V2 now reads as a damaged manufactured survey cabin. Its heavier armored ribs, visible floor sockets, interrupted hull edges and broken cargo fragments resolve the defining V1 mismatch. The score increase follows those visible structural changes, not the additional production effort or polygon count. V1 remains HOLD6.3 and is not overwritten.

## Closure of the four V1 blockers

1. **Cabin mass and attachment hierarchy — resolved at the neutral threshold.** The main uprights now have continuous broad pale armor, substantial blue-gray shoulders and layered floor sockets. Front and quarter views distinguish the supporting ribs from the retained wall sheets. The outline feels related to the approved Explorer/chest family rather than a thin fence kit. The paired three-station arrangement remains coherent.
2. **Roof/hull damage — resolved at the neutral threshold.** Shoulder terminations now have uneven exposed dark frame ends and differing armor retention; the middle image-left rib visibly lacks the same pale sleeve as its neighbors. The rear panels and side upper edges are interrupted by deliberate broad breaks. Their shapes read as damaged framing/sheet material, without stone-like rubble or a mass of arbitrary shards. This still looks cleaner and more regular than the target, but no longer like an intentionally unfinished pristine shed.
3. **Manufactured surface divisions — resolved.** The floor now has a small readable set of large plates; rear wall sections, frame divisions and restrained horizontal panel features supply useful manufactured scale. They avoid the fine texture noise previously cautioned against. The target contains richer stepped panel structure; V2 retains simpler surfaces without losing its identity at the shown scale.
4. **Damaged breadcrumbs — resolved.** The pale panel has a broken, offset outer edge and a visible fold/separation line, exposing the dark underframe. The cargo frame has a visibly broken rail and bent side support. Both remain grounded, subordinate and recognizable as the same manufactured family. These are now plausible wreck fragments rather than intact furniture.

## Preserved practical qualities

- The floor-level entry remains visibly broad and clear. Manifest width is 3.2m. The ramp meets the floor on the neutral plane and does not show an intervening visual wall.
- The roof remains open. The manifest reports 2.8m clear width between upper rib tips, reduced from V1's 3.8m; the stills show the chest clearly through that opening. This narrower aperture must be considered explicitly in native foreground/side camera review, rather than assuming V1's clearance or camera behavior still applies.
- Chest scale and identity are retained. In the sampled fully open view the lid stays clear of the back wall and side ribs, and the interior cavity is visible. This is one static clearance pose, not continuous opening/catch-motion or interaction proof.
- The receiver is supported on a visible, seated dark mount in rear/quarter views. Its simple dish shape remains legible. The amber cartridge-like insert stays in a restrained attached bracket rather than adding floating/greebled equipment.
- No obvious unsupported fragment, impossible floating fitting, reflective surface, emissive clutter or new AI-like fusion is visible. Broad matte ivory and blue-gray planes fit the approved Explorer palette and stylized form language.
- The manifest reports 6,320 triangles, seven draw primitives and 475,600 GLB bytes for the seven components. This is nearly twice V1's geometry and bytes, justified here by visible primary-form improvement; it is still a bounded asset family. These figures do not establish measured mobile FPS or the full scene cost.

## Remaining fidelity gaps, not neutral admission blockers

- The shell is more rectangular and its rib spacing/shoulder angles more regular than the target's battered cabin. Preserve the current structural coherence; any later damage polish should use one or two intentional deformations rather than scattering small jagged cuts everywhere.
- The pale dish still reads as a fairly regular faceted bowl with a missing wedge. A less uniform damaged rim would approach the target more closely, but the current low silhouette, support and manufacturing story are clear.
- The ramp remains a plain shallow plate, while the target gives its threshold more panel definition. One broad seam or restrained edge treatment could improve fidelity later without raising the curb or adding collision complexity.
- Some retained cream side areas remain quiet large rectangles. Their broadness is acceptable at mobile scale; avoid responding with small texture details that would obscure the current clear palette.

## Evidence boundaries and next gate

It supports relative player/module scale and reward prominence only. It cannot pass the native-camera gate.

Root must independently check actual west-facing placement and terrain support, arrival/breadcrumb route readability, entry/ramp traversal, the narrowed open roof and near-wall occlusion at native landscape and relevant portrait views, chest opening/interaction, and truthful shell/fragment colliders. The geometry revision invalidates any assumption that V1 shoulder collision envelopes or visibility automatically remain suitable. No staged-image claim here covers those tasks.

No browser, Blender run, production edit, full test or commit was performed by this reviewer. Only this versioned review and its evidence receipt were written.
