## Actual frame findings

- **Attack now has a deliberate opposing pair.** Front3q.20 lifts the pale muzzle and reveals more of the mint underside, while.43 drops the muzzle below that starting line and pitches the cheek/face forward/down. Side.20 versus.43 makes this change especially clear: the long neck/face axis visibly rotates from raised preparation into a lowered release. The difference is no longer carried mainly by a tiny whole-body impulse, which was V2's actionable failure.
- **Recovery is represented, not proven in time.** Front3q.70 has returned toward the neutral head line without a new extreme or obvious collapse. These stills show preparation/release/return shapes. They cannot establish snap, acceleration, recoil weight, ease-out or whether the release appears at the correct gameplay instant. No spit/projectile or mouth-opening event appears in these isolated model frames; a lowered head alone is not proof of a readable fired attack.
- **Hurt has a separate response direction.** Peak front3q.15 raises and slightly turns the muzzle rather than using Attack's downward release. The near cheek angle changes with the asymmetric head pose. Opposite.15 confirms a raised neck/chest line while the four-leg body remains supported. The lateral component is restrained; Hurt peak still resembles Attack preparation more than it resembles Attack release. Their temporal ordering is therefore essential to reliable discrimination.
- **The Hurt sample is sparse around the hold.** Front3q.15 and.40 look nearly unchanged;.80 returns toward neutral. This can be a coherent held recoil with a return, but these frames cannot tell whether the first impulse is quick enough or the hold feels frozen. Do not infer perceptual timing from the key values in README.
- **Anatomy remains coherent in these poses.** Pale cheek roots remain attached through both attack extremes and opposite Hurt. I see no reopened bright cheek hook/slit, loose dorsal peak, detached shoulder, lost knee volume or paw visibly suspended above the floor. Broad blue body, cyan feet/crest, pale face and continuous paddle tail retain the approved identity. Occluded contacts and intermediate sole slip are not proven by ten stills.

The retained preservation receipt reports exact decoded V2/V3 equality of mesh attributes/indices/weights and Idle/Walk/Run tracks, with only Attack/Hurt changed. I read that receipt and verified the exact candidate hash; I did not independently rerun its decoded-track comparison. Unchanged neutral facets/paddle limitations are not a new action failure.
