# Ironspine Range — bounded implementation brief

This brief translates the selected gully direction into one local structural
pass only after ordinary-input route proof. It preserves the complete R2
corridor, every source/home footprint and the locked camera/HUD poses.

1. **Arrival gully:** make a shallow, terrain-supported pair of side shoulders
   that reads as a sheltered opening from `(-2080,-3160)` while leaving the
   central corridor and lower iron approach open. Do not stamp a rectangular
   canyon or place a freestanding wall over the route.
2. **Mineral focus:** retain `f1:r:-42:-64:4` exactly and expose it from the
   arrival frame through sparse existing stone feet only after full mesh
   projection and support checks.
3. **Ramp continuity:** use two alternating, low terrain shoulders outside
   the R2 corridor to carry the gully toward the interior pose. They must
   remain subordinate to the walking slope, not become climbing gates.
4. **Crown shelf:** keep `f1:r:-43:-61:6` and `:8` in place, frame the upper
   stop with a sparse shelf lip and an open downhill return view; do not count
   two records as two interaction successes until ordinary proof establishes
   both.
5. **Scenery restraint:** use verified transformed `asset_trail_stones`,
   `asset_fen_stone`, and only compatible low vegetation as grouped edge
   texture. Suppression/repositioning occurs only at final desired-record
   selection after existing caps/allocation, with default-world and
   incremental parity evidence.

**Existing roles:** terrain carries relief; existing iron preserves the two
candidate stops; trail/fen stones and sparse vegetation can texture safe
edges. **Conditional gap:** one grounded stratified sidewall/rock form only
if audited kit plus terrain cannot achieve the selected arrival silhouette.

**Deferred:** tall target cliffs without support proof, a new cave/climb or
hazard system, new resource/Wildkin behavior, biome-wide density edits,
lighting/camera changes, and a distinct descent. The full ordinary route,
collision, health, harvest range and reload evidence remains a prerequisite to
source authorization, not a post-hoc check.
