## Implementation interpretation

1. Preserve the major service identities and the uncluttered central route. The existing primitive pod/workshop/sanctuary are recognizable carryovers; this score does not newly admit their models or require their wholesale replacement.
2. Build a small consistent barricade family: broad solid panel, dark supported post, triangular foot, restrained status insert. Do not literally reproduce inconsistent generated fittings. Limited salvage wear is useful; dense scratches, lights and new decorative systems are not.
3. The southern dividing wall is a useful before/after anchor. Its apparent exterior control cannot be the only activation point. Production must provide an ordinary walkable route for the player to reach the yard task, or an opening accessible from the starting apron, while retaining the north exit. Opening and removal must read correctly in the native camera.
4. Keep the large quiet yard center, but establish three unmistakable removable debris bundles inside its accessible task area. The current isolated stones, fringe weeds and left-edge log alone could read as ordinary decoration in an already usable yard. Clearing should leave an obvious change before the new perimeter appears.
5. The four orange-capped stakes communicate a surveyed extension. Their projected image positions are not authoritative plot corners. Follow the production sector/legacy geometry and preserve a starter bench/crate placement bay, service clearances and supported wall feet.
6. The survey camera and spacious framing describe composition, not a replacement follow camera. New walls must be checked from ordinary bounded zoom at the real landscape viewport for hand occlusion, hidden interaction targets and player visibility.
7. The pale hull/panel language can connect the later Survey Module to Camp; alien ruins should keep their different silhouettes. This target does not review the survey wreck, cartridge, pack recipe, helper drone or Rootfall model.
