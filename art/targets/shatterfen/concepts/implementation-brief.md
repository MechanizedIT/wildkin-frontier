# Shatterfen rotation 1 — implementation brief

**Selected target:** `concepts/selected.png` (the exact `return-hummock-direction.png` copy).

## Matching pose

Use the frozen native destination pose recorded by `baseline-captures.json` and compare against `baseline-destination.png`. Do not derive placement from the target image or change the portrait camera to imitate it. Arrival/interior remain evidence poses for circuit continuity; overhead remains a diagnostic only.

## Five implementation priorities

1. Use the existing terrain owner to form one shallow, connected channel *cue* around the destination hummock, preserving a wide dry central lane and every protected source approach and Tidefin home/leash disk.
2. Keep the target's room hierarchy: a gently raised dry middle, lower wetland edges, and a visible southwest return opening. Prove terrain and hull support at the real portrait pose before freezing any source result.
3. Replace local uniform scatter only at the cue's edges with small, irregular grouped existing reeds, lilies, stones, and low plants. Leave the pedestrian lane and forage approaches visibly open.
4. Use existing Tidefin and wetland scenery as framing from dry ground; do not move, respawn, or add wildlife, and do not add deep water or a new reflection/water system.
5. Preserve sampler order/caps, exact resource IDs and transforms, alternate-world neutrality, source support, and route clearance; evaluate actual native captures rather than prop counts.

## Asset/system mapping

Existing wetland scenery and the current terrain/scenery owners provide the intended low-edge language. Existing Tidefin is presentation context only. No asset is admitted by this brief. A narrow single asset may be proposed only through the separate gap review if full-kit and native-projection evidence show that existing reeds/lilies/stones cannot create the selected silhouette.

## Deferred aspirations

Reflective water, water shader work, swimming/wading, bridges, wide ponds, new resource types, and wildlife behavior changes are outside this visit.
