# Shatterfen rotation 1 — independent target selection

## Selected reference

`selected.png` is an exact copy of `return-hummock-direction.png`. It is the stronger feasible direction because it uses the existing portrait destination composition: a dry central hummock, one broad shallow channel cue, grouped reeds/lilies/low rocks at its boundary, and an open return lane. It improves the currently flat, uniformly scattered destination without requiring a literal reflective pond.

The unselected `arrival-channel-direction.png` gives a useful wetland mood, but its broad reflective water surface and reflection treatment would imply a water/rendering system that this visit does not authorize. Its grouping and edge contrast may inform composition only.

## Target fitness and limits

The selected art retains the portrait HUD/player relationship as an aspiration and uses a legible central room. It is not a terrain, support, route, wildlife-disk, or asset-admission proof. The actual baseline shows a much flatter seeded field and large Tidefin framing; any structural work must use the frozen native destination camera, preserve resource approaches and wildlife homes, and verify full mesh projection before source edits.

Water reflection, deep channels, swimming/wading, bridges, new resources, and new Wildkin behavior are deferred. The selected target is attainable only as a shallow terrain-and-prop composition using existing wetland forms, unless a separately reviewed single missing asset is later demonstrated necessary.
