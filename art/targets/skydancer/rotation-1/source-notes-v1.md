# Skydancer rotation 1 — target source notes

`skydancer-construction-target-v1.png` is a direction-only construction sheet.
It is **not** an orthographic measurement, a replacement recipe, or a visual
admission. The target preserves the current bipedal bird identity: cream
faceted head and torso, gold beak, turquoise wings, split turquoise tail, two
legs, and two folded wings.

## Observed baseline evidence

The four input views are the current baked factory runtime baselines:
front, left, rear, and three-quarter. They show separated narrow wing layers
and thin leg/foot joins. The target changes only the inferred three-dimensional
connection and readable volume of those forms: compact attached folded shoulder
masses, overlapping feather plates, connected bent legs, grounded talons, and
a readable two-strand tail root.

## Inference limits

Generated feather contours, the unseen wing-root surface, leg depth, and talon
shape are proposals for a later independent anatomy plan. They do not establish
exact dimensions, triangle counts, collision, animation, wing span, or
additional behavior. Native dimensions remain the source-audit measurements;
all existing gameplay, save, placement, and collider contracts remain frozen.
