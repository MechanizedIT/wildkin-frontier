# Skydancer rotation 1 — independent construction-target review

## Decision: PASS as a direction-only anatomy target

The four views give a coherent bipedal cliff-bird read: two compact folded wing masses attach at the upper torso, plate overlap is consistent front-to-rear, the split tail has two rooted strands, and the legs/talons meet the ground. This directly addresses the measured baseline failures—side-on wing thinness and fragile-looking leg/foot joins—without changing the recognizable cream, turquoise, gold, and split-tail identity.

The sheet is not a measurement source. Its apparent wing thickness, head/body proportion, plate count, talon size, and hidden roots must not override the actual baked-envelope and authoring measurements. A numerical plan must preserve exactly two folded wings, two legs, two taloned feet, and two tail ribbons; retain the current head, torso, beak, eyes, tail identity, species IDs, collision, placements, and gameplay. It should create full-surface wing-root and leg/foot attachment evidence, keep all changed geometry inside the frozen actual envelope unless a separately justified contact-safe exception is approved, and prove source-to-baked parity at serializer precision.

This target does not authorize mesh, bake, collision, animation, or gameplay work. Native close-pass evidence remains required because the frozen collision box is smaller than the visual envelope.
