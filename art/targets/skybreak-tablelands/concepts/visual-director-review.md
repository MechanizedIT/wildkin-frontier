## Fidelity and feasibility

The selected image preserves the source-correct Skybreak Tablelands HUD, the 412×915 portrait projection, player scale, and the settled crown pose: player near `(12,-228)`, yaw `-2.1815223`, normal 36° portrait camera. It is a quality direction, not a literal asset list: the large number of perimeter boulders and highly continuous rock shelves are more developed than the admitted low-prop kit currently proves.

Keep the other locked views in the same implementation/review set: arrival `(4,-153)`, yaw `0`; ascent `(-7,-184)`, yaw `-0.5404195`; return `(36,-188)`, yaw `π`. Arrival/ascent/return retain their source-correct Heartwood HUD; crown retains Skybreak HUD. Target work must not homogenize that transition.

## What the target requires

1. A compact crown hierarchy: open supported center around the Mossling home, a visually lower/broken outer rim, and a distinct east reward shelf/outlet.
2. Broken, unequal rock planes and low groups at edges, not repeated vertical curtains or evenly scattered grass.
3. One crown frame in which Mossling, its three cloudflowers, crystal-side reward, and exit are all readable at their fixed relative locations.

The existing `asset_cloudflower`, `asset_trail_stones`, `asset_fen_stone`, `asset_pebble_cluster`, `asset_fen_reed`, and `asset_mushroom_ring` can supply bounded edge accents. Existing crystal, Mossling, resource identity, cloudflower clearance, support disk, route anchors, and east-stone beats remain protected. Current evidence does **not** authorize a new asset automatically. If an existing-kit structural blockout cannot establish a sufficiently readable crown rim, a single small rock-outcrop role may be proposed through the separate reference/mesh/review route; it is not part of this selection.

Deferred: literal tall enclosing cliffs, blanket boulder fields, a new cave/bridge/tower, a new plant family, moving the crown residents/reward, regional-label changes, camera/HUD/light changes, and any route or gameplay redesign.

## Decision

**PASS for the frozen implementation brief and one bounded structural visit.** Target selection does not prove the 3–5-minute ordinary route, interactions, reload, streaming, support, or mobile performance.
