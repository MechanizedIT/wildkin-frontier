## Brace and access obligations

The current supports suggest grounded A-frame/X-braced timbers and keep their feet out of the lane. In the correction, retain exactly two substantial pieces per bank, with one intelligible rope joint per pair. Every upper bearing point should visibly meet retained wood, not a leaf cluster or an apparent detached bark plate. Place feet on real near-side ground with enough approach space for the player to perform the brace action. Avoid adding a crossbeam or low diagonal through the newly opened route.

The drawn open lane looks broadly usable, but an image alone cannot establish 3m world clearance or return-landing space. The eventual model must derive closed collision and the open cut faces from one assembly and remove only the center blocker. Braces, roots and banks must remain outside the physical corridor. Preserve a reachable south-side tool position in CLOSED and a safe standing/return position beyond the opening in OPEN. Neither the pictured gap nor a low-poly mesh check proves those contacts.
