# Emberglass Caldera — implementation brief

## Five bounded priorities

1. Build two unequal low terrain shoulders: a stronger left shoulder that curves into the rear and a smaller broken right shoulder. They must frame the interior without becoming walls.
2. Keep the central charcoal floor and the full four-meter breach lane broad, dry, and visibly continuous from arrival through the mineral approach.
3. Use the existing `asset_ember_spire`, `asset_ember_bloom`, and `asset_pebble_cluster` only as sparse outer-edge clusters that reinforce each shoulder. Preserve fixed staged identities and ecology contracts.
4. Preserve the blue crystal at `(846.5,-1976)`, iron at `(854,-1980)`, and the Emberhorn home at `(850,-1986)` as readable choice points with clear approach/retreat space. Do not alter rewards, behavior, or encounter rules.
5. Use existing Caldera terrain color ownership for restrained charcoal/rust shelf contrast and project the full proposed geometry through the frozen arrival, interior, and destination cameras before source work.
