## Verdict

**HOLD — 7.5/10 target fitness.** Composition/state geometry 1.9/3, lighting 2.7/3, materials/style 2.6/3, details/consistency 0.3/1. The format is improved, but the same closed/open geometry contract remains inconsistent. Do not model the two panels as separate objects or admit V2 because a correction was attempted.

## Improvements that are visible

- The square sheet now gives each scene roughly native landscape aspect (~2.2:1), with separate caption strips and useful approach foreground. The gross V1 shallow-crop problem is fixed.
- Player position, foreground stone arrangement, broad outside root mass, right branching and principal banks correspond more closely across the two states.
- The biological style remains strong: broad matte umber/moss-teal plates, coherent woody mass, restrained exterior foliage and pale end grain. The scene still represents a raised grassy ridge, not an invented drainage cut.
- Open-state feet/lashings and retained wood contacts are visually intelligible, and the supports stay out of the central lane. The intended clear ground passage remains readable.

## Remaining blockers

1. **The center piece is still not the piece removed.** CLOSED marks are approximately x530 and x715; OPEN faces are approximately x490 and x760 at the same 1254px width. Even allowing for the visible thickness of the marks/faces, the final gap is substantially wider than the marked segment. Both retained ends have been shortened beyond their cut marks. This repeats V1's central failure and cannot be excused as perspective, since the pair is intended to share one fixed camera and unchanged outer pieces.
2. **CLOSED still looks girdled or already severed.** Pale bands remain broad and nearly continuous over the visible trunk. The image does not clearly retain top/back bark or an intact core around limited front-facing starter-tool notches. A modeled shallow fibrous notch can be made coherent; tracing these apparent full rings and then changing the cut plane cannot.
3. **Aspect ratio is repaired; native scale/framing is not yet matched.** In a visual comparison, the V2 Explorer is about150px tall, while width-scaling the original roughly60–65px native figure from844px to1254px would give roughly90–97px. The familiar foreground stone cluster is also substantially larger. These are approximate image observations, not a computer-vision measurement, but the difference is clear. The reference still presents a closer view than the baseline. Do not change the accepted camera, player scale or field of view to reproduce it.

The style and broad topology are not the reason for HOLD. The image remains useful as an art-direction study; it is not a consistent production state-pair target.

## Change the reference approach

**Yes: one unambiguous CLOSED native-aspect reference plus a fixed geometric removal contract is adequate for the next production-reference gate.** It would avoid asking image generation to independently recreate the same trunk in a second state. This changes how the state is specified, not the required identity, style or correctness standard.

Use this bounded approach rather than another almost-identical two-panel correction prompt:

1. Create one CLOSED landscape view using the actual baseline framing and the same approved Explorer scale. Keep this biological identity: flared left rooted end, asymmetrical tapering right branches, broad layered bark, low grounded banks and a thick center that visibly reaches the lane floor. Show only restrained partial front stress/notches, or keep ambiguous cut marks out of the beauty image and define them precisely in the companion geometry specification.
2. Define a single local/world coordinate frame and two explicit cut planes in the accompanying layout contract. For the intended approximately3m lane, a proposed pair could be local X=-1.5 and +1.5 relative to the lane center, subject to actual envelope/collision validation. These are modeling constraints, not dimensions inferred from generated pixels. The two planes must define both the removable piece and final end faces.
3. Build one coherent trunk mesh/assembly with permanent left/right parts and a center bounded by those planes. Derive OPEN by removing that exact center and exposing the matching cut faces. Preserve all permanent vertices/transforms, bank rocks and branches. Do not generate a separate open trunk or deform the retained ends to enlarge the gap.
4. Add the two near-side timber support pairs as separate grounded pieces with explicit upper contacts and one lashing per pair. The V1/V2 brace depiction can remain a qualitative construction reference; its target positions must respect the actual same-mesh end faces and clear lane. No independent generative OPEN beauty frame is required to define those measured supports.
5. Judge fresh exported CLOSED and OPEN model views under the same locked native-style camera against the closed reference and the explicit removal/support contract. The review must verify fixed retained geometry, cut-face correspondence, support joints, grounded feet and clear passage. Then separately test actual runtime state, collision, interaction approach and return space. If gameplay has an intermediate cut-but-unbraced state, its visuals and collision must also agree; the two beauty states do not settle that behavior.

This can proceed only after the single closed reference is itself independently fit for purpose. It is not permission to waive current identity requirements, skip model state-pair review, accept hidden walls in a visibly open path, flatten the ridge or grant owner approval by inference.

## Boundaries

Preserve both V1 and V2 as held studies. No further same-pattern image generation, production change, browser/Blender run, full test or commit was performed by this reviewer. This verdict covers reference fitness only; it provides no model/native/phone acceptance.
