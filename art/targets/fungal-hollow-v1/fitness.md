# Fungal Hollow target fitness review

## Selection: lock low-poly V2 — 8.7/10 target fitness

The current `target.png` is the stronger production target. Lock it without a third generation pass. Its broad teal floor, angular olive banks, unequal depth layers, open route and repeated purple accents are materially attainable with the current flat-shaded terrain and admitted kit. The previous `target-textured-study.png` remains a useful mood study but is rejected as a literal production target because its moss, wet soil and botanical detail exceed the renderer and asset catalog.

Use V2 for **floor/bank shape, broad color areas, depth and visual fullness**. Use `inside.png` as the authoritative truth for the portrait camera, HUD, player/Mossling presentation and the exact complete silhouettes of admitted cluster models.

## Locked exclusions

1. The individual generated mushrooms are placement shorthand. Production uses the complete canonical Mooncap ring model; it does not split that model into invented single caps or add new mushroom variants. Match the purple rhythm and foreground/midground/rear distribution with whole clusters.
2. The terrain facets express broad teal and olive vertex-color regions. They do not require new textures, moss/soil detail, new shaders, or per-prop materials. Current lighting and flat-shaded material behavior remain authoritative.
3. V2's nearly bare route is not a requirement to remove all existing ground dressing. Retain a bounded amount of admitted low tufts while suppressing the tall generic reed dominance in the strong Fungal outing. Physical support, the four-metre route, resource pocket and two exits override decorative matching.

The very tall left stone and steep banks are composition goals only at scales that pass the existing full-footprint support and collision checks. Under these exclusions, V2 is a credible target for production and a clearer fit than the textured study.
