## Findings

1. **Retired course assets are removed.** I find no launch disc, checkpoint ring, box pedestal, platform sequence, ladder/rail course or comparable timing apparatus. The two small stones and subdued salvage on the eastern shelf read as ordinary geological/dressing elements, not a jump course.

2. **The water correction works.** The west pool and outlet read as one calm, continuous water feature with shoreline rocks and rooted banks. The previous cascade/white-foam implication is absent. Implement it as the existing same-level low water plane; no elevated-water behavior is implied by the image.

3. **The upland loop is plausible as a target.** A broad route leaves the low southern/eastern toe, follows the long side of the cliff mass through unequal terraces, and the upper route bends back north-west toward the central low approach. The corresponding descent reads separately rather than as a platform sequence or a circular maze. Broad shelf tops and exposed faces make the intended 3m, 6m and 8m occupancy legible enough to block out. The image cannot measure the grades, widths or continuous capsule support, so the numeric landform contract and elevation/native-pitch proof must own those details.

4. **Rootfall is visually contained enough for this planning image.** The closed root remains a north-centre obstruction with rooted/rocky flanks, and the contour network appears to rejoin south of it. The high eastern mass must still terminate on the south side in production: verify no walking or ordinary-jump route reaches the destination side before repair. This target is not proof of that guard.

5. **Anchor drift is non-blocking under the clarified boundary.** The painted Survey, arrival marker and north beacon still sit lower/nearer the image edges than the previous normalized guides. Record any resulting terrain/route adaptation against the real Survey, Rootfall, landing, trigger and return transforms. Do not move source anchors to follow the picture, and do not request more image generation solely for this pixel drift.

## Implementation reading

Use this image for its three-part composition and terrain silhouette: a quiet west habitat, clear low salvage/travel spine, and one eastern high-ground loop. Keep the real Survey roofless/west-open apron flat and accessible, make the actual 3/6/8m transitions broad and graded, and put resources/observation pockets where a player can approach and read them. Limit foliage to visible edge groves and preserve clear harvesting space. The optional natural jump(s), if retained, require measured reach and visible recovery terrain; they are not authorized by any image feature.

This PASS authorizes no world edit by itself and establishes no native playability or physical-phone acceptance. It closes the V1 visual-target blockers; after the blockout, review the clean overhead, anchor overlay, side/elevation profile, and locked native-pitch route views against the actual source transforms.
