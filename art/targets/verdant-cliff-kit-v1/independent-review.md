# Verdant cliff kit V1 — independent target review

**Verdict: PASS — 8.4/10 as a future scenic-cliff modeling target.** This is a useful, distinct gray cliff-mass family for the locked Verdant Upland target. It adds broad grounded geology rather than another blue, pointed silhouette, and it remains clearly separate from harvestable ore.

## What works

- The sheet has exactly three readable roles: a low stabilizing toe, a tall shelf/ridge break, and a squat ledge. Their silhouettes are substantially different without becoming three unrelated material families.
- All three forms visibly meet the ground. The central split and the right ledge are supported by continuous stone masses; I do not read floating fragments or a required separate attachment.
- Large matte planes, subdued warm gray/charcoal separation, and restrained recess growth fit the approved faceted language and the existing upland's gray cliff borders. There is no blue crystal/spire reading, shine, glow, collectible color cue, or decorative ore-vein language.
- The broad toe and ledge will break up the current long, planar cliff faces in the native overlook without competing with its trees, chest, or route affordances.

## Modeling direction / limits

Treat each pictured object as **one complete grounded scenic mesh**, including its apparent supporting rock; do not implement the central split, lichen strips, or right-hand support as loose floating pieces. Keep the lichen/recesses broad and sparse, or omit them where mobile distance makes them read as a resource seam.

The middle buttress should stay broad-bottomed and blunt-crowned when rotated: preserve its stepped strata and deep vertical break, but avoid a thin rear peak or silhouette that turns it into a spire. The right form needs a visibly solid underside/support at ordinary player pitch; retain the overhang as a ledge, not an arch/cave. The left toe should remain low enough to dress cliff feet without hiding harvest nodes or suggesting an interactable deposit.

The reference is plausible for a small mobile kit at roughly **1,500–1,900 triangles total** with simplified unseen backs and roughly 8–20 deliberate planes per main mass. That is an implementation estimate, not a polygon count or certification of a model that does not yet exist. Avoid copying the image's many tiny face transitions literally; spend geometry on the three exterior silhouettes, the middle split, and the ledge underside.

## Remaining art caution

The target's beige-lit studio palette is slightly warmer/lighter than the live native overlook. Match its *gray/charcoal mass hierarchy* in production while tuning the albedo toward the established Verdant cliff gray, so placed pieces do not look like imported desert rocks. This PASS is limited to target fitness; it does not certify placement, collision, mobile rendering cost, or a future 3D asset.
