# Emberglass Caldera target review

**Target quality: 8/10 — lock with the constraints below.**

The proposed image is a coherent and appealing baseline-derived direction. It preserves the portrait play camera, player readability, existing HUD language, central travel lane, and low-poly rendering style while giving the destination a clear volcanic identity. The charcoal floor, rust-red shoulders, asymmetric dark spires, warm Cinder Bloom accents, separated crystal and iron rewards, and one distant encounter form a readable foreground-to-midground outing. It is substantially more useful than a generic volcano image because it shows the intended breach funnel and practical gameplay choices.

The scene is attainable with the admitted kit when read as a composition, palette, and fullness target:

- `asset_ember_spire` can supply the near black silhouettes, but its native height is 3.53 m. At the planned scales it reaches roughly 4.1–5.3 m, so terrain must supply the broad solid shoulder and rim mass. The target's largest edge walls are terrain silhouettes, not enlarged blocking spire props.
- `asset_ember_bloom` and `asset_pebble_cluster` can supply the orange accents and dark rubble. Their repeated target rhythm is useful, but the implementation should use bounded clusters rather than reproduce every illustrated pebble or orange shard. Blooms and pebbles remain nonblocking dressing.
- The admitted crystal and iron node match the target's separated mineral-choice read and retain their existing harvestable colliders. The central lane must remain open before and after either node disappears.
- The shipped Emberhorn is smaller and anatomically different from the generated bull-like figure. The target locks its warm color, supported midground position, separation, and encounter prominence only; it does not require new anatomy or scale beyond the existing creature contract.

The image shows more distant ground and rear enclosure than the normal 42-degree follow view can reliably frame. For implementation comparison, judge the first 5–20 m: an open charcoal lane, a strong near-left shoulder/spire, warm edge accents, the mineral choice, and a readable Emberhorn or bowl cue. The full ring, far rear crest, and long illustrated corridor remain movement/orbit/overview reads under the revised contract.

The mottled charcoal and rust ground should be interpreted as the existing terrain color pipeline with broad low-frequency variation. It does not require a new complex texture, lava, smoke, dynamic glow, lighting system, generated creature model, or UI redesign. Existing UI differences in the target are outside the art lock.

With those constraints attached, the target is specific enough to lock for the three-pass terrain, composition, and polish loop. It does not need another target revision before production.
