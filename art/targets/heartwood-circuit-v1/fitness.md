# Heartwood circuit target fitness

**Result: CONDITIONAL FIT — 7.3/10.** This is a usable composition reference for a fuller starter route, but it is not a feasible pixel-match target for the authorized placement-only pass. It does not reach the project’s 8/10 art-admission threshold and is not final production visual admission.

## What is feasible

Compared with the sparse native R2 baseline, the target communicates a modest, practical improvement: two staggered canopy silhouettes, a readable reed/flower group, scattered low accents, and open walking floor around the player. The existing fitted blockouts already reproduce most of that spatial idea in the departure and clearing-exit views. Candidate scope of seven added canopies and 21 added lows, within 44 near / 52 total / 18 canopies, is sufficient to pursue the same three-beat route composition with existing assets. Keep native terrain, camera, HUD, lighting, models, colliders, and the shared 640-cluster grass cap unchanged.

The target only proves one authored-looking portrait moment. It does not prove the whole clearing-to-encounter-to-forage circuit, open traversal, encounter/resource readability, streaming cost, or native presentation from all three required views. Production should be judged against the actual blockouts and route, using the target only for silhouette density and asymmetric grouping.

## Differences that must not become requirements

- **Models/geometry:** the target redraws canopy crowns and trunks, enlarges and reshapes the lily pad/flower, changes reed blades and flower heads, and invents brighter angular rock clusters and extra stones at plant bases. These are model hallucinations; reuse the current tree, reed, lily, cloudflower, trail-stone and pebble assets without mesh changes.
- **Palette/materials:** it shifts the ground toward a brighter mint gradient, deepens canopy greens, warms trunks, brightens flowers and rocks, and smooths the native faceted materials. Preserve the native palette and materials.
- **Lighting/rendering:** it adds softer higher-resolution shading, stronger ambient/contact shadow treatment, bloom-like softness, and cleaner antialiasing. No lighting, postprocessing, renderer, DPR, or camera change is in scope.
- **UI:** every HUD surface is regenerated: scale, spacing, fonts, borders, minimap contents/arrow, objective panel, joystick, buttons, companion portrait, hotbar icons and counters differ from native R2. Preserve the actual HUD exactly; judge scenery around its real occlusion footprint.
- **Grass:** grass is larger, denser, more clustered and differently shaped than native shared ground cover. Do not hand-place target grass or raise the 640-cluster cap. Any fullness that depends on this grass treatment is excluded from the achievable match.

## Production constraints

Use the target for canopy depth, low-group cohesion and preserved open floor only. Require each of the departure, starter encounter and west-forage views to show at least two distinct near/mid canopy silhouettes and one coherent low group without forming a corridor wall or hiding harvestables, Mossling, Tidefin or Emberhorn reads. Retain all route/resource/wildlife clearances, exact existing identities and starter-only selection budgets. Reject or thin placements if the native result needs new meshes, lighting, UI, grass density, terrain/camera changes, or exceeds 44 near / 52 total / 18 canopies.
