# Sunscar Desert rotation 1 — visual director selection

**Selected target: `selected.png` (copied from `target-destination-v1.png`).** It is the stronger direction because it turns the existing north crystal into a readable destination within the normal portrait view: an open approach, one compact mineral cleft, and spaced pale return-side forms produce a useful foreground/midground/background sequence. The arrival direction contributes a compatible idea—the framed open basin—but its monumental dark obelisk is not grounded in the packet or current kit and would pull attention from the mineral route.

The selected image preserves the baseline's player scale, HUD layout, turquoise crystal identity, broad centre lane, and normal portrait framing. It is direction only: the extra pale ribs, clustered subsidiary crystals, and denser stone margins are not evidence that current source placement, support, clearance, or resource protection can reproduce them.

## Asset decision

Verified existing roles from the packet/data are `asset_crystal`, `asset_iron_ore_rock`, `asset_trail_stones`, and `asset_fen_stone`; they can retain source identity and furnish small pocket-edge stone texture. Their full transformed silhouettes have not yet been rendered against the target, so they are not evidence that they match its warm, eroded sandstone rib. First audit and render those plausible assets plus existing rock/outcrop recipes. If none gives a continuous, grounded, warm low rib at portrait scale, authorize **one bounded reusable eroded-rib asset**; do not substitute a generic name or create a full desert kit.

Deferred: new heat/survival rules, new enemies, a cave, global terrain reshaping, a lighting/camera change, uniform desert density, and any Camp-to-Sunscar travel claim.
