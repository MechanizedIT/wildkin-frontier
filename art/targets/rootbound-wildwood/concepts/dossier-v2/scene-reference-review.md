# Rootbound portrait scene references — independent review

## Orientation Meadow v1 and Root Galleries v1

Both images are valuable **scene-reference** candidates. They establish a consistent warm rootwood/cool shade language, clear pale-floor contrast, low-poly faceting, and readable edge density. Meadow v1 makes the orientation role especially legible through its large quiet floor and dark forward rootline. Galleries v1 gives the desired asymmetric heavy root/trunk edge, grounded log and small mushroom contact while retaining a visibly broad central passage.

Neither is a matching native-camera target or a runtime placement design. In both, the player sits closer to 60% than the specified 56% frame height and is oversized/foregrounded relative to the recorded Rootbound portrait framing. The deep corridor perspective, large framing foliage, full-height trunks, and high enclosing edge masses are more cinematic than the documented 36°-down, 52°-FOV, ground-dominant capture class. The root forms should not be copied as route-crossing geometry; the current traversal intent allows a deliberate, proven climb/clear pocket, but does not make every impressive ledge or harvestable an approved transition.

### Reuse

- Retain Meadow’s pale open floor, edge-only fungi, dark eastward rootline cue, and contrast between reset space and denser transition.
- Retain Galleries’ single side-root hierarchy, warm/cool material separation, log-and-fungi contact ecology, and clear travel-width intent.

### Limits for later targets

Use both as `provisional-camera-class` references only. A matching acceptance target needs a newly recorded native pose or the existing Arrival/Destination/Crown-threshold capture, the player at the actual recorded scale/roughly 56% height, verified HUD-safe bands, and projection/support/clearance proof. Future depiction may show a visually signalled optional climb or real harvestable-clear entrance only after its specific access and return proof is defined.

## Lantern Grove, Thornstone Verge, and Heartroot Crown v1

Lantern Grove v1 usefully isolates a log-end fungus family and leaves a generous central interaction floor. It misses the desired damp/shaded hollow hierarchy: the floor is very bright and broad, the canopy edge is generic, and the strongest purple caps are larger than the restrained contrast called for. Reuse the grounded log/cap contact and clear center; reduce cap scale, move the visual weight into a cooler sheltered edge, and keep the return gap visible.

Thornstone Verge v1 has a good clearing-facing escape lane and shows that the mineral side should remain edge-bound. Its grey rocks are too few and too generic to read as a Thornstone geological family, while the large tree, cut log and floral cover dominate the supposed mineral cue. Reuse the lane asymmetry; replace the current isolated boulders with one low irregular shard family, and keep any root/log framing outside the route.

Heartroot Crown v1 supplies the warm litter, old-root palette, quiet margin and return-facing open floor that the Crown needs. Its huge hollow walk-under arch is neither covered by the selected buttress-root constituent nor feasible as an immediate 4–6m root-collar translation. Preserve the broad joined base and warm value contrast as a **future separate Crown landmark brief**; do not treat the arch as a current asset requirement or traversal gate.

All three continue the player/camera mismatch already found in Meadow/Galleries: player is near 60% frame height with a cinematic vertical corridor and heavy foliage frame. They remain scene references, not frozen native targets. Overall art direction is strong; Lantern and Crown are useful with constraints, while Verge needs a more distinct mineral-family reference before its image should direct a production asset.
