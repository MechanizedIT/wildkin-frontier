# Rootbound dossier-v2 scene references — consolidated implementation brief

These five images are art-direction references, not placement, collision, terrain, route, or runtime instructions. Match actual portrait poses only where one is recorded; new Gallery and Verge work remains provisional-camera-class until native poses and projections exist.

## Five implementable priorities

1. **Keep the Meadow floor as the reset space.** Preserve the broad pale, low-density floor and a dark eastward edge/rootline cue. Existing clearing palette and low foliage/log roles can express it; avoid foreground canopy curtains and route-crossing roots.
2. **Build one edge-bound root hierarchy for Galleries.** Use the reviewed buttress-root constituent only after raw/cleanup admission. It must occupy a side band, leave a 4–6m intended lane, and read as joined collar/bent roots rather than a walk-under arch. Existing fallen logs and low fungi may gather at its shaded contact edge.
3. **Compose Lantern as a cool damp edge around an intentionally dry center.** Map existing `asset_fallen_log` and `asset_mushroom_ring` to one grounded log-end cluster; reduce the scene references’ oversized violet caps and avoid glow/water. Retain the central interaction/return gap.
4. **Give Verge a real low mineral family.** Existing `asset_fen_stone` may be tested as a low grouped edge seam, but the scene reference shows an unmet identity gap: it needs several varied, connected-in-read grey-green shard forms, not a single generic boulder. Keep the escape lane clear and bind any optional climb/clear discovery to its own proof.
5. **Treat Crown as a later landmark constituent.** Reuse its warm litter and old-root value band now; defer the huge hollow arch. A separate Crown construction target must establish a grounded low collar, scale, camera projection, support and route role before a new asset loop.

## Deferred aspirations

Dense tree walls, evergreen canopy blankets, big walk-under root arches, magical fungal glow, water, added quest/discovery systems, generic decor destruction, and emergency evacuation are not part of this scene-reference set. Optional climb or harvestable-clear access is allowed only as a separately named, verified local transition with a return path.
