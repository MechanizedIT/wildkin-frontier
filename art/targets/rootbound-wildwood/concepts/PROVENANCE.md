# Rootbound Wildwood portrait target candidates

These are two unselected visual direction candidates for a restarted habitat
pass. They are references for an independent director and a later structural
brief; they do not approve or reopen implementation by themselves.

## Candidate A — Root Gallery / Heartroot proxy

`root-gallery-heartroot-target.png` was generated from the actual R2 gallery
portrait frame as the primary composition reference. Owner concept images 1
and 4 informed the restrained root-gallery mood only. Its prompt retained the
existing mobile HUD, camera pitch, player/companion scale, and low-poly game
grammar. It asked for a central broad walking lane framed by close root-ridge
banks, with a practical nearby canopy-tall/canopy-spread Heartroot proxy and
one fallen-log arrival cue.

## Candidate B — Lantern Hollow / Thornstone side seam

`lantern-hollow-thornstone-target.png` was generated from the actual R2
destination portrait frame as the primary composition reference. Owner concept
images 2 and 3 informed its sheltered-hollow and ecological-cluster direction.
Its prompt retained the same mobile HUD, camera, player/companion scale, and
low-poly game grammar. It asked for a pale safe floor, a limited two-ring plus
fallen-log bank cluster ahead of the player, and a distinct two-thornstone side
opening with no new mechanics or hero assets.

## Source set and limits

- Actual gameplay frames reviewed before generation: `r2-arrival.png`,
  `r2-gallery.png`, `r2-destination.png`, and `r2-overhead.png` under
  `art/reviews/rootbound-wildwood/`.
- Owner concepts reviewed before generation: attachment images 1 through 4
  supplied with the overnight direction.
- The source prompts explicitly prohibit cinematic horizons, portals, bridges,
  caves, buildings, runes, glowing crystals, new creatures, mechanics, or an
  additional asset pack.
- These candidates deliberately simplify the reference mood into the current
  feasible kit: canopy-tall, canopy-spread, fallen log, mushroom rings,
  fen-stones, and ordinary low plants.

No selected verdict is recorded here. The independent director chooses a
candidate or writes a combined brief before any restarted implementation.
