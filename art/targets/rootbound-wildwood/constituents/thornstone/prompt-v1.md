## Exact prompt

Create one high-quality 3D game asset design target for "Rootbound Thornstone", an original grey-green weathered mineral outcrop for a stylized low-poly woodland mobile RPG. Single isolated complete object on plain light warm-gray background, soft neutral studio light, sharp focus, three-quarter elevated view about 25 degrees down. No environment, no text, no labels, no scale cube, no ground tile, no decorative loose objects. The outcrop should fill 80 percent of the image with all edges visible and a subtle contact shadow.
Design intent: a squat wide connected bedrock foot supports THREE unequal angular stone teeth, not identical cones or crystal prisms. The main tooth is a broad twisted wedge, leans 15 degrees outward, has a broken oblique crown and a stepped shoulder; secondary tooth is about 60 percent as tall and points another direction; third is a low thick split plate. Deep but finite fracture creases expose warm charcoal-gray interiors, broad planar fresh breaks, softened eroded older edges. Connected grounded substantial volume, no thin spikes, no floating shards, no tunnels. Whole proposed envelope about 1.6m wide by 1.1m deep by 1.45m tall; interpret visually, do not print measurements. Matte desaturated slate-gray faces with olive mineral staining and only two or three warm rusty root-imprint scars across lower faces. Scars are shallow mineral patterns, not actual wooden roots. Distinct material regions broad enough to reproduce, rich modeled silhouette and sculptural mass, moderate detailed chips on dominant edges, no noisy photoreal microtexture. No glowing veins, gems, metallic material, moss, fungi, vines, foliage, skulls or magical runes. Believable low-poly geology with deliberate asymmetric silhouette and scale hierarchy. Beautiful clear production reference that can support either image-to-mesh or a professional Blender construction plan. It should look like harvestable woodland stone, not a decorative crystal or miniature mountain.
