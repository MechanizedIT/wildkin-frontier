# Independent Thornstone reference review — CONDITIONAL KEEP

**Reviewed:** `reference-v1.png`, `prompt-v1.md`, the Rootbound final-destination capture, Thornstone dossier, and Wildwood spatial/scene-reference plans.

## What V1 usefully establishes

V1 is a strong high-detail geology study for an uneven, grounded mineral family: a broad low base, unequal leaning teeth, broken crowns, stepped shoulders, and restrained olive/rust mineral variation. Those traits distinguish a local Thornstone seam from the current two identical, narrow `asset_fen_stone` cones visible at Destination. Its forms can be constructed as one connected opaque mineral mass with three dominant teeth, a thick split plate, and finite creases; no floating shards, glowing seams, or separate crystal language are needed.

Keep V1 as the detail/material master. Treat its high-frequency fractures, chips, and mottled rock response as sculptural reference only. They are too realistic and dense for the existing faceted mobile palette, and the output contains substantially more interlocked rock mass than the prompt's simple three-tooth hierarchy. It is not a direct game-size, placement, collision, or harvesting specification.

## Required companion target before construction

Create **one simplified stylized sibling reference**, then choose between it and V1 for the construction plan. It should preserve V1's wide grounded foot and three unequal tooth silhouette, but reduce the object to a clear connected low-poly volume with: broad plane groups; two or three deliberate charcoal fracture bands; only sparse olive/rust lower-face stains; no photoreal chip noise; and a 1.6m-wide / 1.1m-deep / 1.45m-high *design envelope*. Include a three-quarter view with all base contacts visible and no foliage, loose stones, or ground tile.

The eventual Verge placement must remain an edge-side optional cue with a clear escape lane. It may not copy V1's dense rock wall, enclose the player, or silently treat the existing Fen-spire props as already equivalent to this distinct mineral silhouette. Ground support, transformed hull, camera projection, protected footprints, and any shared-stone economy remain later gates.

**Status:** V1 retained as a high-detail master; construction **HOLD** pending the one stylized sibling and independent selection. No model, runtime asset, or mineral gameplay change is implied.
