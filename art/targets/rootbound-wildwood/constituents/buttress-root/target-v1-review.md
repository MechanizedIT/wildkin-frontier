V2 should be selected independently against this image for three visible qualities: a thick joined collar rather than roots merely meeting the trunk, four-to-five broad grounded buttress directions that frame a lane, and a fork that bends before splitting. The eventual selected image still needs an explicit scale/projection contract before modeling.
