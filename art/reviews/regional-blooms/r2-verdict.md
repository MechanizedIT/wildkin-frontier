# Regional Blooms R2 visual verdict

**Composition: 2.1/3**  
**Lighting: 2.4/3**  
**Materials: 2.5/3**  
**Details: 0.6/1**  
**Total: 7.6/10 — HOLD**

R2 fixes the dominant R1 scale failure: the crystal, flowers, and fen stones now read at a credible gameplay size, every major silhouette is visible, and the open approach remains clear. The result is a coherent crystal pocket, but it still reads as a staged shrine rather than a rare natural growth. The two fen stones form a wide mirrored pair, the three flowers form an evenly spaced triangle, and the small trail groups sit near the screen edges instead of visually binding the pocket. Compared with the locked target, the core and rear stones sit slightly too high and the elements have too much empty space between them; the target reads as one denser cluster.

Lighting and materials remain consistent with R1. The admitted assets are clean and legible, while the real directional shadows are harder and the crystal is darker teal than the target's soft mint treatment. Those are locked-target differences rather than reasons for global lighting or material work. The target's large foreground trail-stone anchor around the player is still absent, reducing depth and exact screenshot similarity.

A third bounded pass is worthwhile. Pull both fen stones, side flowers, and trail groups roughly 15–25% inward; move the core/rear grouping slightly toward the player while keeping the front flower near its current screen position. Break the bilateral read by setting one fen stone farther back, bringing only one side flower closer to the crystal, and rotating the trail groups unevenly without closing the approach. Restore the existing foreground trail stones if the bloom clearance removed them. Keep the current asset scales, camera, HUD, player, lighting, and models.
