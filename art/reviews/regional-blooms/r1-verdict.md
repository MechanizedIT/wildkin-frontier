# Regional Blooms R1 visual verdict

**Composition: 1.5/3**  
**Lighting: 2.4/3**  
**Materials: 2.5/3**  
**Details: 0.6/1**  
**Total: 7.0/10 — HOLD**

R1 establishes the intended pocket with the admitted crystal, flowers, fen stones, and a clear sandy approach, but the real assets are substantially larger on this fixed camera than the locked target predicts. The crystal dominates the upper third at roughly twice the target's apparent height, the left fen stone clips against the screen edge, and the roughly 10 m arrangement loses the target's compact, fully framed ring. The target's two side trail-stone groups are consequently weak or outside the frame. The unchanged in-engine light is directionally compatible, although its harder, longer shadows and the crystal's darker teal faces cannot reproduce the target's softer mint treatment exactly. Original model materials remain coherent and readable.

The highest-value R2 change is a bounded transform revision: reduce the core and flowers to roughly 60–70% of their R1 apparent size, reduce the fen stones slightly less, and compress the lateral span toward about 7–8 m while retaining the open center approach. Keep the pocket centered at its current depth; its front edge already lands near the target's normalized vertical position. Pull the clipped left stone inward and rotate/offset the two rear stones and flowers slightly so the result reads as a seeded natural cluster rather than a formal shrine. Also preserve or restore the baseline foreground trail stones around the player if their disappearance is caused by the bloom recipe; they are part of the locked target's depth framing. No camera, global lighting, HUD, player, or new-model change is warranted.
