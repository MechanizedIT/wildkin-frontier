# Regional Blooms R3 visual verdict

**Composition: 2.4/3**  
**Lighting: 2.4/3**  
**Materials: 2.5/3**  
**Details: 0.7/1**  
**Total: 8.0/10 — PASS**

R3 is the strongest usable candidate. The inward outer placements and closer core now read as one bounded destination instead of R2's disconnected row of props. The crystal has a clear harvestable focal role, the side trail groups visually contain the pocket, all admitted silhouettes remain legible, and the approach stays open. The uneven depth and spacing reduce the shrine effect enough that the group can read as a seeded natural pocket in ordinary portrait play.

The composition still carries visible art debt. The two fen stones remain a conspicuous paired backdrop and the three flowers retain a loose triangular rhythm, so the pocket feels partly arranged rather than fully organic. The core and fen stones sit slightly higher and taller in frame than the locked target. The target's large foreground trail-stone anchor around the player remains absent, which weakens exact screenshot similarity and foreground depth.

Lighting and materials are unchanged from the prior rounds: the canonical assets are clean and coherent, but real directional shadows are harder and the crystal is darker teal than the target's soft mint rendering. Those differences do not justify broader lighting, material, camera, or model changes. Select R3 and preserve these perceptual gaps as visual debt; a fourth composition loop is not warranted.
