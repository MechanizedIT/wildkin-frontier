# Skydancer R2 — independent visual review

## Decision: HOLD

R2 materially improves the front silhouette: the wings now descend and extend farther from the torso. The intended defining read still does not arrive. Across the right and three-quarter views, each wing reads as one solid dark-teal swept slab. The three cover plates remain buried within, or visually fuse to, the primary wing surface; their folded-feather cadence is absent at 48 px and only weakly inferable at full scale.

### One final R3 repair

Keep the R2 primary-wing envelope and all lower anatomy. Rebuild only the three existing plates per side as an exterior shingle chain: each root face remains buried in the wing, while each plate’s free body is offset beyond the primary-wing outer surface and terminates at a distinct descending tip. Prove nonzero external surface clearance and visible projected separation in locked front and three-quarter cameras before build. This changes the failed method from overlapping/fused solids to visible closed exterior layers; it must not add a fourth wing, new feathers, material trick, or runtime change.
