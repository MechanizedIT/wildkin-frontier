# Skydancer R1 — independent visual review

## Decision: HOLD

The native build honors the approved literal V2 construction and the reported shell/topology checks explain why the wing roots, legs, and plates remain mechanically coherent. That is not perceptual admission.

The target asks for broad, descending, visibly layered turquoise wings. In the front, side, three-quarter, and 48 px views, R1 instead reads as narrow, near-horizontal dark-teal loaves. The three plates merge into a shallow rim; their layered cadence is not legible. The cream body, head, beak, legs, feet, and tail retain the intended identity, but the defining wing silhouette remains substantially short of the target.

### One focused repair

Revise only the primary wing and its existing three closed plates per side. Use the available frozen authoring envelope to give the wing a materially deeper downward outer fold and stagger the three plate tips vertically and longitudinally, with visible separation in the locked front and three-quarter views. Preserve the two-wing anatomy, torso-root containment method, legs, feet, tail, and all runtime contracts. A new numerical plan must prove the revised shell/plate attachments and projected front/three-quarter plate separation before a counted R2 build.
