# Skybreak ecology R2 — independent visual verdict

**HOLD — 5.8/10** (PASS requires at least 8.0)

- **Composition — 1.5/3.** R2 improves the crown grouping and makes the blue crystal a readable anchor in the overhead and overview captures. The lowland thickets remain too small and too close to cliff edges to survive normal overview framing, the east-return stones do not read as a route rhythm, and the portrait capture still presents two Mosslings on mostly bare ground with no visible flower/crystal habitat frame.
- **Lighting — 2.2/3.** The inherited exposure, shadows, and mint/sand palette remain coherent. Most plants still share the ground's light value, so their forms disappear at gameplay distance; only the crystal keeps strong separation.
- **Materials — 1.7/3.** Saturated crystal blue and the denser crown color flecks improve resource recognition. Berry, leaf, flower, and stone surfaces still occupy too little visible area to carry their roles outside the overhead view.
- **Details — 0.4/1.** The crown has a more convincing mixed patch than R1, but the route mouths and return path still lack the target's layered, repeated clumps.

**Largest gap for the final pass:** recompose the existing instances for the normal portrait camera. Pull each lowland thicket away from cliff occlusion and consolidate it into one broad, tall silhouette; move the crown flowers and crystal into the Mosslings' visible portrait frame as one compact habitat landmark; then place three clearly separated existing stone/plant beats down the east return. Judge the result first in portrait and overview—overhead density alone is not enough.
