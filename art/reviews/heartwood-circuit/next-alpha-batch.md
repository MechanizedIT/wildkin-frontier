## Recommended next batch

Build one **Ironspine ascent and sheltered-gully outing** near the fixed `ironspine-range` site at `(-2100,-3000)`. It advances an allocation-only habitat, uses the existing strongest uncustomized height vocabulary, and moves the alpha toward geographic variety rather than another plant-density pass.

The terrain identity should be a bounded local profile composed through the existing `frontierRegion` → `frontierTerrain` path: one jagged spine, two or three broad supported mineral shelves, and a lower sheltered gully. Give the player two legible choices that reconnect: a shorter exposed ascent with fall/Emberhorn pressure and a longer gully route with safer footing. Use slopes, connected shelves and open sky; no caves, overhang claims, general climbing changes, hazard framework or repeated Skybreak stamp.

Reuse admitted assets deliberately: `asset_iron_ore_rock` and `asset_crystal` as real finite harvestables; Fen/trail stones and pebble clusters for shelf edges; sparse existing canopies/fallen logs only in the sheltered gully; Ember spires only if their volcanic reading remains subordinate to dark mountain rock. Reuse ordinary grass/scenery batching and regional tint/scale. Do not add a new asset family unless an actual-view blockout proves the admitted kit cannot distinguish the route.

The outing payoff is reliable iron plus a smaller crystal reward for existing Fabricator/Resonance construction, reinforced tether, and field/health/pickup upgrades. A fixed territorial Emberhorn can guard the exposed shelf through the current wildlife owner. The route stays completable by dodge and ordinary harvesting; Tidefin Ward reduces approach risk and Emberhorn Cragbreaker accelerates the clustered mineral job, making existing companions useful without becoming keys. No new species, loot ledger, quest, resource type or ability.

## Focused proof and playable witness

Source tests should prove deterministic feature zones; smooth height/color transitions at positive and negative edges; one continuous supported ascent and return; full support beneath mineral and creature footprints; route clearance from solid scenery; fixed IDs and bounded residents; mineral partial/depleted unload/rebind and reload; and no output changes at representative Heartwood, Skybreak, Fungal and Caldera samples. Use current streaming/lifecycle suites rather than duplicating them.

One native portrait witness should start at the recognizable lower gully, walk by ordinary controls to the fork, take the exposed ascent, read and avoid or Ward through the Emberhorn warning/charge, harvest the iron/crystal shelf (optionally compare Cragbreaker), descend by the gully, and retain exact pack/depletion/health/atlas state after literal reload. Record scenery/terrain resident maxima and the worst boundary frame. The visual decision asks whether the spine, shelves and sheltered return route read in normal play; a top-down map alone cannot admit the habitat.
