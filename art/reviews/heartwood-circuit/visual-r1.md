## Improvement and guide fidelity

- **Improvement over native baseline: CLEAR PASS.** `starter-production-r1.png` replaces the broad empty upper field in `starter-r2-baseline.png` with a readable tree/reed/lily landmark while preserving a large player floor. `forage-production-r1.png` similarly turns the nearly blank forage baseline into an identifiable gathering grove with several depth layers and an open approach.
- **Native guide fidelity: PASS for the three matched witnesses.** Starter and forage closely retain their blockouts' silhouettes, low-group locations, open center, and UI clearance. `departure-trunk-first-contact.png` is effectively the clearing-exit guide in production: the close trunk, reeds, lily, stones, player, and Wildkin maintain the intended readable cluster. The separate departure guide is not directly matched by a supplied production frame, so its apron-to-distant-group continuity remains unproven in this visual review.
- **Fullness and naturalness: PASS within existing assets.** Asymmetric clumps and uneven spacing avoid a planted grid. Repetition is visible, especially in the tall pink reeds and identical canopy forms, but does not make the route feel artificial at this density.
- **Gameplay readability: PASS in the supplied stills.** Open ground remains obvious, the player and companion are legible, and the groups read as landmarks rather than corridor walls. The reported seven real trunk colliders and native contact pass support physical credibility; still images do not independently prove every route/resource/wildlife clearance.

## Three consequential remaining gaps

1. **The route lacks the target's continuous depth and ground rhythm.** Large open areas are still visually quiet between authored clusters. The 640-cluster grass cap and placement-only boundary explain much of this, but the difference remains visible.
2. **Native lighting and materials flatten separation.** Foliage, ground, reeds, stones, and trunks share close values and hard-edged shading, especially in mid-distance. The target's softer fill, stronger contact treatment, and broader color/value range make equivalent density feel richer and easier to parse.
3. **Asset repetition limits ecological specificity.** Reused canopy crowns and tall pink reed groups dominate multiple route beats. The arrangements are credible, but the route does not yet achieve the target's apparent variety in plant scale, shape, stone dressing, and localized micro-detail.

## Future visual debt

Closing the generated-reference gap requires work outside this placement batch: camera framing, lighting and shadow treatment, material/value separation, grass presentation, and greater mesh/prop variation. HUD differences in the generated target are excluded and should not be treated as scenery debt. Those changes need a later authorized visual slice rather than being inferred as requirements for this batch.

The stated source result (52 residents, 18 canopies, 28 new records), seven real trunk colliders, and native contact pass support production integration. The 180-frame walk profile (33.3 ms median, 35.5 ms p95, 117.6 ms maximum with one unattributed hitch) is useful desktop/native evidence, but it is not phone admission; the hitch and portrait-device performance remain future validation debt.
