# Heartwood first-circuit audit

Status: read-only recommendation after the fresh builder outing and the retained 20-prop Camp clearing. The saved `starter-approach.png` and `forage-approach.png` prove a long, weakly marked field between the clearing and useful forage; they do not prove a traversal defect.

## Why the route is sparse

- `src/world/frontierScenery.js` deliberately protects any residency containing a staged starter chunk. `isProtectedSceneryResidency()` disables dense `f2c:i:*` infill and `selectFrontierScenery()` uses the legacy limits of 18 near specs and 34 total rather than the inland 2,304/2,312 limits.
- The current `(0,-2)` residency publishes 26 specs: 18 near plus eight outer canopies. Eleven near specs are staged: three canopies at `(4.85,-78.5)`, `(1.8,-85)`, `(6,-106)` and eight small lows packed into `x=5.2..9.2, z=-75.8..-80`. Seven near specs are seeded ordinary detail. The only seeded canopy is far west at `(-18.15,-89.27)`; most other seeded detail is also well off the northbound portrait axis. Current exact selected-ID hash is `0c740db352a0f532b203b5c85fe0433002a55531fcde4d3e175d0cc50af886b6`.
- Raising the general Lush canopy chance is not an effective first fix. An ordinary recipe attempts at most six specs and one canopy per chunk; protected selection admits at most four non-staged near canopies, and seeded centers can remain outside the view. Enabling dense infill would weaken the intentional starter/Skybreak protection and reintroduce the much larger streaming budget.

This batch turns the already working gather/return/build loop into an unaided first circuit. It does not count Heartwood as a new completed terrain grammar and does not add a discovery, resource, species, road system or runtime owner.

1. **Cap displacement:** assert 17 staged near IDs, 18 total near, eight outer, 26 total, no `f2c:i:*`, at most 12 canopies, deterministic reversed-residency output, and update the protected starter hash intentionally. Failure means new landmarks silently evicted one another or expanded the runtime budget.
2. **Crowded gameplay sources:** for every new full scaled footprint, retain the existing route, all neighboring forage and all wildlife-home assertions. Assert every proposed staged record is actually emitted; staged admission is individual, so an unsupported record otherwise disappears without making the group atomic.
3. **Portrait readability:** use the existing normal portrait camera from recognizable clearing/approach positions and require one landmark or grouped silhouette ahead in each of the three route moments, while the floor route and harvest prompts remain unobstructed. Source tests establish placement safety, not on-phone readability or unaided navigation.

## Stronger alternative: one complete starter-only composition

The six-spec option is mechanically safe but is unlikely to make all three 5–20 m portrait depths feel full. The admitted canopies are only about 3.25–5.54 m wide and 3.82–6.23 m tall; one added crown cannot frame a roughly 50 m journey. The low kit is about 1–2.8 m wide, so isolated lows will still read as specks. A meaningful blockout should instead test three asymmetric thickets, each with two or three staggered canopy trunks and roughly five to eight mixed lows, while reusing the current mid/far staged trees. A reasonable first fixture is about seven additional trees and 18–24 lows, distributed across departure, starter encounter and forage views rather than evenly along the route.

The strict 18-near limit is the real blocker. The smallest code owner remains `FRONTIER_SCENERY_CONFIG` and `selectFrontierScenery()` in `src/world/frontierScenery.js`: add a starter-only selection budget recognized from the fixed opening stage chunks `(0,-2)`/`(0,-3)`, leaving generic protected coast and Skybreak values unchanged. A bounded candidate is 44 near, 52 total and 18 canopies for this starter window only. With seven new trees and 21 new lows, the existing 11 plus 28 additions yield 39 fixed specs, leave five near slots for ordinary lows, and retain eight outer canopy silhouettes. This is still far below dense inland residency and uses the existing recipe cache, visual builder, 12.5 m low-prop cells, ground-cover cap and collider publication. Exact counts should follow the native blockout; they are a ceiling and composition hypothesis rather than an admission promise.

The cost is bounded with the current kit. The measured four-tree Camp set is about 14,288 triangles/eight draws, so seven additional external-model canopies are roughly 25,000 triangles/14 draws. A 21-low reed/lily/cloudflower/trail/pebble mix is roughly 10,000–13,000 triangles; cell-and-asset instancing caps it at 21 draws and should group it into fewer. Ground cover remains one draw and at most 10,240 triangles/640 clusters, although more staged owners can make it reach that existing cap earlier. A conservative added envelope is therefore about 48,000 triangles and 36 draws including a fully charged ground-cover batch; likely cost is lower. Seven real canopy trunk surfaces are the only new colliders if Fen stones are omitted.

Before source admission, a private native blockout must measure all three exact ordinary views: leaving the accepted Camp clearing around `z=-45`, crossing the starter shelf around `z=-75`, and approaching the proven west forage around `z=-92..-96`. Each frame should contain at least two distinct near/mid canopy silhouettes plus one coherent low group, without hiding the next open floor or harvestable. Then walk gate-to-forage-to-Camp without coordinate guidance, contact both sides of the real trunk boxes, trigger the existing resources/encounters, and record scene calls/triangles plus scenery rebuild time and frame median/p95/max. Reject or reduce the fixture if it creates a corridor wall, obscures Mossling/Tidefin/Emberhorn reads, drops any saved resource/home, exceeds the proposed 44/52/18 starter budget, or introduces a visible streaming hitch.
