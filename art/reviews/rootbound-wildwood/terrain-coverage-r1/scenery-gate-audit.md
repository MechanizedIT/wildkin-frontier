# Rootbound scenery gate audit

`rootboundVisibleScenery` currently suppresses every ordinary canopy and every ordinary low except the four radius-6–8m circles at Arrival, Gallery, Lantern, and thorn. This explains why the 200×200 Rootbound allocation remains bare between curated islands.

Current generated selector output across Rootbound circuit chunks is recorded in `scenery-gate-audit.json`. The minimal follow-up is not a new generator: retain all four pockets and existing curated IDs, but let the already admitted **non-solid low** records survive when their centers fall in continuous Gallery, Verge, or Crown shoulder polygons and outside existing full route-clearance. Keep ordinary canopies suppressed. Existing `admitScenerySpec` remains responsible for terrain support, fixed sites, forage, wildlife, and world gating; stable ordinary candidate IDs remain unchanged.

This is a recommendation only. It does not claim a terrain height change or alter the current runtime filter.
