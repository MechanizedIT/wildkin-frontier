## Structural grammar

- A western, north-to-south root ridge frames the rich Root Gallery.
- A second, curved east ridge creates the safer route edge and its sightline.
- A shallow Lantern hollow sits south of the Crown, with its floor clear for
  an ordinary existing tree or fiber interaction.
- Thornstone stays in the south-east seam only; it does not become a
  Rootbound-wide scatter family.
- Curated scenery makes three to five patch families around those forms while
  keeping the clearing and route lanes open.

## Protection contract

Terrain changes are finite and return their pre-change height at every current
forage or wildlife support footprint in the expanded `x=-550..-300`,
`z=525..775` envelope, with a smooth safety feather. The Rootbound module owns
the static default-world footprint table, so terrain does not import ecology or
wildlife and create a sampler cycle. The structural test fingerprints every
existing source ID and transform in that envelope, including its one-chunk
border, and checks regional-place catalog parity. Curated scenery uses the
existing exclusion recipe before admission; it never replaces ordinary forage,
wildlife, groves, or their saved identities.
