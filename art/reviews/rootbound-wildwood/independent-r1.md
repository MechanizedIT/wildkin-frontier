# Rootbound Wildwood R1 — independent habitat review

**Scope:** raw runtime R1 capture review against the Rootbound packet. The captures are not judged from resident counts, the destination target, or exploratory route timing. The documented 39-second baseline route is comparison context only; a frozen ordinary-input route and reload are still pending.

## Decision: HOLD — structural repair required

R1 usefully removes much of the original even meadow scatter and introduces a darker bounded terrain treatment. It does not yet make the ordinary portrait player feel they have entered a Rootbound Wildwood. In the three player views, the terrain reads mostly as open dark floor with sparse existing props; the overhead shows two faint brown strokes and a large dark square rather than rooms, routes, or a landmark hierarchy.

**Overall: 3.9 / 10 — HOLD.** Critical axes remain below 6.5. This is a perceptual HOLD, not a correctness verdict.

## Three consequential gaps

1. **The structural grammar is invisible from normal play.** Ridges/hollow appear primarily as a dark overhead block or ground facet change, rather than roots and rooms that frame the player’s path.
2. **The circuit has no readable navigation story.** The arrival, gallery, and destination frames could be three stretches of the same sparse field. The Heartroot proxy and safe/rich choice are absent from the player’s decision space.
3. **The destination ecology lacks a concentrated payoff.** The Lantern Grove does not yet look damp, fungal, sheltered, or rewarding; its few surviving props do not form an anchor-scale cluster.

## Consolidated R2 repair packet

Make one structural composition repair only; do not add another habitat, increase broad resident caps, or generate a generic hero tree.

1. Reposition/retain the admitted canopy-tall, canopy-spread, fallen-log, mushroom-ring, and thornstone kit so the **Heartroot proxy** is visible high-center from both arrival and gallery, while a paired root-ridge/hollow edge frames rather than merely recolors the route.
2. Shape one visible **wide safe floor lane** toward that proxy and one denser side entrance toward the Lantern Grove. The difference must be readable through actual clearance, framing, and landmark placement in portrait, without a quest arrow.
3. Concentrate the two mushroom rings and lantern log at the hollow’s sheltered edge, then put both thornstones together at the opposite verge. Preserve open center clearance and use the existing kit only; correct the destination ground framing so slope facets support the hollow rather than becoming its dominant visual feature.

Recapture matched arrival, gallery, destination, and overhead views after this one repair. The next review must receive a frozen ordinary-input enter → choose route → useful interaction → stopping point/return story plus literal-reload evidence before performance/lifecycle or traversal can score above provisional.
