## Three consequential gaps

1. **Camera-space grouping remains absent.** R2's Destination objects are closer in world terms, but the normal camera still reads a log and thorn props set apart on a bare plane. The mushroom-ring portion of the left shelter is not perceptually present.
2. **No room wall or shallow pocket is visible.** The repair retained open clearance, but the removed steep thornbank has not been replaced by a low, traversable visual boundary. The color shift behind Gallery does not create enclosure or direction.
3. **The circuit lacks a connected reveal sequence.** Arrival's tree, Gallery's shrubs, and Destination's props each stand alone. They do not lead the eye through orientation → enclosure → choice.
