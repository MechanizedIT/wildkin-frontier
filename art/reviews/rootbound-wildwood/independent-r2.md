## Decision: HOLD — visual repair budget exhausted

R2 makes the tall canopy proxy more apparent in the gallery and reduces a few R1 clutter conflicts. It does not solve the decisive normal-play problem. Arrival is now almost entirely empty floor, the gallery’s tall trunk is clipped by the top HUD/frame, and destination remains a sparse sloped surface with a stump and two small tree clusters. The overhead suggests a central intervention, but it still does not translate into root rooms, a Heartroot focal point, or a visible route decision at portrait scale.

## Improvement and regression

- **Improvement:** the gallery now exposes a larger existing-canopy proxy, and the overall item density is lower than baseline/R1.
- **Regression:** arrival loses even the limited foreground framing R1 had; it reads more like an empty green field. The gallery proxy is placed too high for its role and is swallowed by the HUD/top crop. The destination remains structurally unconvincing despite clearer ground facets.

## Repeated consequential gaps

1. The authored terrain changes do not read as Rootbound structure through the normal portrait camera.
2. The circuit still lacks an on-screen landmark and readable safe/richer route decision.
3. The Lantern Grove and Thornstone Verge remain absent as distinct ecological payoffs in their normal-play frames.

No further cosmetic or placement repair is recommended this run. A later attempt needs a different structural method: first validate the normal camera’s projected landmark and room geometry against actual asset bounds, then author the route/clearance and terrain mass around that evidence before placing decorative ecology. Preserve R2 as the final honest visual HOLD rather than expanding this batch.
