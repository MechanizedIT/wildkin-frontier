The current “5–7m composite” sentence cannot yet be checked against the Crown, Gallery return, resource/home exclusions, or mobile frame.

Keep low dressing deliberately sparse until that structure is visible. No target-likeness, per-prop polish, or extra asset gate is required for this amendment.
