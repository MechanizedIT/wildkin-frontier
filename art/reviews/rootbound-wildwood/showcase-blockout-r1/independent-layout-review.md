The runtime records show the intended groups are resident, but the five matching portrait views do not yet establish five rooms. Meadow changes almost imperceptibly. Galleries remains an open field with one oversized dark slab entering from the upper-left. ## One coordinated repair: portrait shoulder bands

Keep the same five zone keys, route, collision policy, and existing/placeholder asset rule. Reposition and group the **existing blockout masses**, after projecting their full bounds through each recorded pose, into these screen-space bands:

- **Meadow:** leave the central/lower half open; make one single far shoulder enter the upper third as the departure cue. Do not spend a new group here.
- **Galleries:** make west-rib and east-rib appear together as opposing upper-frame shoulders, leaving a broad vertical sightline through the centre. The north collar should close only the far end of that sightline, rather than enter as a wall from one edge.
- **Lantern:** preserve its already readable low pocket and use it as the open, damp break between the two larger shoulder sequences.
- **Verge:** consolidate the two placeholder thorn groups into one asymmetric, receding one-sided seam in the upper/mid frame; it should read as several angular rises, not a square screen-facing block.
- **Crown:** overlap crown-base and both wing groups in the far upper third into one 5–7m connected U/root-and-canopy silhouette behind the approach. Keep the lower-middle reveal clear and do not place a dark rectangular face across a route edge.

For this repair, use simple joined low-poly root/leaf volumes with a neutral earthy/green placeholder tint so their mass is legible; no mesh refinement, new TRELLIS work, or small-prop pass is needed. Before source changes, record each group’s projected full bounds at its matching pose and require that its intended shoulder band is visibly occupied outside the normal HUD. Afterward, recapture the same five views plus overview and judge only region rhythm, route visibility, and Heartroot read.

The current pass has no evidence of blocked route or save break, but it also has no visual basis to call the whole-region shape successful. Preserve the useful Lantern result and the blockout records; replace the layout, not the asset pipeline.

## Coordinated repair feasibility

**GO for this single layout repair.** Twenty to thirty-five low, non-colliding instances arranged as a few named recipe groups is proportionate for a broad visual blockout. It directly fixes the prior failure mode—one primitive per zone—without reopening individual asset work.

Use no new canopy collision trunks by default. The visual leaf shoulders, rootweb, mineral seam, and Heartroot wings should all be non-colliding; introduce one existing trunk collider only if an implemented landmark requires it and its full lane clearance is then checked. The Crown rootweb must be one overlapping 5–7m assembly, not three visibly separated logs.

The revised Meadow/Galleries/Crown poses are the right way to remove the old world-left assumption. Before capture, project each whole group at its matching pose and place it in the intended shoulder band; that is a group-level framing check, not a per-prop gate. Capture the same current/baseline pairs and overview after the repair, with the central lane visually empty.
