## Heartroot ingredients and hull

`heartroot-crown` starts as a temporary composite: one existing rootfall center/rootfall braces for a 5–7m broad root base, one existing spread/tall canopy trunk seated on a supported flank, plus two visual-only low-poly leafy/root wings. The collision hull is **only** the existing canopy trunk plus the selected rootfall bearing core; all wing geometry has no collision. Its clear lane is a 6m corridor centred on the final spine from `(-452,696)` to `(-480,720)`, widened into an 8m reveal around `(-468,717)`. Rootfall/support, source/home, and camera projection are checked when this group is implemented; this blockout does not assert those exact assets have already passed there.
