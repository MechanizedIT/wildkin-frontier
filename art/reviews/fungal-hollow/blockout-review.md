# Independent Fungal Hollow blockout review

## Verdict: HOLD, but feasible with the admitted kit

The current blockout is a useful density proof, not yet a distinct Fungal Hollow portrait. Compared with the baseline, it adds a clear stepping-stone thread and concentrates purple Mooncaps, but almost all authored mass remains in the upper `29-291px` of the frame. The objective panel covers much of the upper-left composition and the inventory covers the upper-right. In the middle and lower portrait, the baseline's tall green reeds and pale flat ground still dominate. The result reads as a fuller Lush/Shatterfen patch rather than a new habitat.

The existing kit is sufficient if the branching rootwash terrain supplies the habitat-scale silhouette. Uneven banks and branching hollows can make the place structurally different from the Caldera and ordinary Lush terrain; Mooncaps, dark logs and fen stones can then identify one inhabited shelf. No new hero asset is essential for this first habitat pass. The low kit by itself cannot carry the identity from its present distant placement.

## Smallest consequential placement repair

Keep the 23-prop budget and the open sinuous route. Redistribute the existing instances across depth instead of adding density:

1. Move one compact **left foreground crescent** about `4-5m` south: mush-a, log-a and two pebble accents should begin around z `-1239.5` to `-1242`, with their centers kept roughly `3.1-3.6m` left of the route. Controlled left-edge cropping is appropriate here. This gives the portrait a low/middle silhouette near the player while leaving the right side open for the proposed resource pocket.
2. Move stone-a from the rear crown to roughly z `-1244.5` to `-1246`, after resampling its full support footprint. Keep stone-c rearward and stone-b on the opposite deep shelf. One near stone, one middle/deep stone and one far stone will read as an irregular ruin line; the current three all peak in or immediately below the HUD band.
3. Leave only two or three Mooncap groups in the far band around z `-1248` to `-1252`. Shift the remaining right-side Mooncap or log into the middle band around z `-1243` to `-1246`, clear of the resource pocket. The target should be foreground/midground/far depth, not two distant side ranks.

The rootwash bank toe should enter the view around the same near-left crescent, then bend away from the route. That lets the mushrooms silhouette against a 1.5-3m bank instead of relying on their roughly one-metre height to fill the screen.

## Palette hierarchy available now

Use the baked palette through asset selection; the current spec path has no per-instance tint contract.

- Make purple Mooncaps the repeated accent and dark logs/fen stones the large value shapes.
- Keep trail stones as a quiet route rhythm and pebbles as base detail. They do not signal Fungal identity at portrait scale, so they should not occupy the strongest screen positions.
- Suppress the ordinary tall reed treatment inside the strong Fungal outing envelope. The foreground reeds are currently the largest readable flora and pull the image back toward Shatterfen. Retain scattered baseline flora outside the shelf rather than clearing the whole habitat.
- Let the terrain owner provide a darker, cooler moss/soil field on the banks while preserving the existing three-grammar truth. This should be a broad ground value behind the fixed-color purple caps, not a new mesh material or prop family.

The repair can remain entirely within admitted assets and the existing terrain/scenery systems. Native capture should judge whether the near-left crescent and one lowered stone create a readable foreground-to-bank diagonal without narrowing the four-metre route or covering the resource pocket.
