## Three consequential gaps for R2

1. **Make the near and mid banks visible from this exact ordinary portrait view.** Raise or move the closest bank shoulders into the lower side edges and carry at least one unequal cut through the middle distance, while preserving the four-metre supported center route. The capture must visibly read as travel inside an eroded branching hollow, not a flat clearing surrounded by props.
2. **Separate the teal floor from olive bank faces using the existing vertex-color/material path.** Strengthen broad color and value bands on visible slopes and shelf edges, with enough faceted shading to show elevation. Keep the locked no-new-textures/no-new-shaders constraint.
3. **Reshape the admitted dressing into asymmetric depth groups.** Thin the most uniform Mooncap perimeter bands and dark tufts in the central lane, then concentrate whole clusters at near-bank anchors, midground shelf turns, and one rear focal group. This preserves fullness while revealing the route, bank silhouettes, and target's foreground/midground/rear purple rhythm.
