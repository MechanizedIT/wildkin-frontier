## Three consequential remaining gaps

1. **Force a visible near-bank silhouette in the ordinary portrait frame.** At least one bank/toe must occupy a meaningful lower side edge and continue into a clearly elevated midground shoulder, with an unequal opposing cut. Adjust visible height/profile or the outing's ordinary supported capture position as needed; a side monolith is not a substitute for readable terrain elevation.
2. **Restore a distinct teal travel floor against olive bank faces.** Use the existing vertex-color/material path to create broad, contiguous color/value regions that follow visible elevation. The current nearly uniform olive plane conceals both the route and the bank geometry.
3. **Open the route and place density on the landform.** Reduce dark tuft noise through the center lane and break the Mooncaps into asymmetric near, turn, and rear groups anchored to visible shelf edges. Preserve all whole clusters, but let the supported route and branching terrain organize them instead of forming an even border.
