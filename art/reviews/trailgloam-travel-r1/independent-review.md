# Independent Trailgloam travel-fixture plan review

**GO for the isolated translated-motion diagnostic.**

The scope is appropriate: it consumes the retained fitted GLB through the existing creature, model-runtime, animation, and Rapier owners, while keeping species registration and production tuning untouched. The fixture states its two non-authoritative speeds clearly. The authored 0.212121 m/s follows the 0.14 m stroke over the 0.66 s stance interval; the 0.875 m/s generic-rusher comparison is correctly framed as stress evidence, not a Trailgloam balance decision.

Using a generic capsule only to validate the movement path is an honest limit. It cannot prove a fitted collision body. Likewise, temporary Loaded-as-idle is explicitly temporary and avoids inventing a finished idle claim.

For useful sliding evidence, preserve contiguous stance windows per foot and report world-space sole drift after accounting for the creature translation, plus absolute height and travel direction. Matching side and three-quarter captures, live clip/rate traces, actual grounded progression, transition checks, and the two-instance independence/removal check are sufficient for this bounded fixture. Any future animation or weighting change should be based on an observed drift or clipping defect.

This GO does not admit runtime Trailgloam placement, collision, AI, save behavior, or a finished locomotion set.
