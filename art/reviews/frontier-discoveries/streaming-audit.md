# Frontier streaming audit

The September 13 native baseline measured the first walking residency boundary at about 608.2 ms in scenery, 90.8 ms in terrain, 34.9 ms in ecology, and 2.2 ms in wildlife. Scenery was the immediate owner because each 5x5 rebuild expanded overlapping 3x3 forage and wildlife clearance neighborhoods repeatedly. The current residency-local cache reduces the representative far-window workload from 333 to 49 recipes per domain (666 to 98 combined) while preserving selection and clearance output.

Remaining exception and commit hazards belong to later bounded slices:

- `frontierChunkRuntime`: resident groups and Map entries are installed one at a time before the complete strip reaches physics. If a later chunk construction throws, earlier new residents remain visible without their collider batch and retry skips those IDs. A terrain slice should prepare detached residents, dispose the staged set on failure, and publish the complete 25-resident snapshot only after one successful physics commit. Initial load, resume, and teleport must still synchronously establish support before queries or movement; normal walking may prepare a bounded incoming strip from the existing animation loop.
- `frontierEcologyRuntime` and `resourceSystem`: ecology records the new residency and removes outgoing chunks before all incoming recipes and placements succeed. A failure can leave a partial window that the same snapshot will not retry. Each outgoing and incoming chunk can also trigger its own Rapier step. An ecology slice should stage every incoming recipe first, apply one resource-owner replacement batch, and refresh broadphase once.
- `frontierWildlifeRuntime`: a residency change clears the source cache and records the new residency before all nine source chunks are sampled. A sampling failure can leave partial cached state, while ordinary one-chunk movement redundantly resamples six overlapping chunks. A wildlife slice may retain a bounded 3x3 recipe cache and publish it after successful selection; capture checks must remain immediate and the four-resident cap unchanged.
- `createPhysicsWorld.updateTerrainSurfaces`: remove/add mutation is not transactional if Rapier throws partway through the batch. The scenery runtime now disposes its rejected new visual and preserves its old visual/specs/residency for retry, but the physics owner cannot currently roll back a partially applied batch. Any general transaction work belongs to a focused physics lifecycle slice and must preserve the single broadphase refresh after a complete batch.

The authoritative loop intentionally runs terrain, scenery, ecology, and wildlife streaming before movement. Resume support is also loaded before the shared support query. These readiness rules prevent holes and should remain intact; no second loop or asynchronous streaming framework is required.
