# Cinderjaw rotation 1 — independent R1 visual review

## Decision: HOLD; one focused R2 anatomy repair is warranted

R1 is a real improvement over the frozen baseline. The former blade legs are now closed bent volumes, the claws stay grounded, and the twelve plates no longer form the baseline's visibly floating shelf. The creature retains its broad dark skull, cream jaw, tail, low stance, and all protected parts.

It still misses the selected target's heavier planted reptile silhouette. In side and three-quarter views the new legs read as narrow, angular hanging struts with small feet rather than short load-bearing limbs. The top/rear plates are orderly and seated, but in profile they read as isolated thin pyramidal points instead of a restrained low armour run. At 48px, the animal reads, but the leg and plate corrections contribute little to its identity.

## One consolidated R2 repair packet

Keep every protected component, the four claw centres, baseline envelope, and Cinderjaw gameplay/collider contract exact. Rebuild only the permitted legs and plates from the V2 attachment data:

1. Make each limb visibly weight-bearing by widening its upper and knee cross-sections toward the torso/inboard side, with a short broad shoulder mass that enters the torso and a deliberate taper only near the unchanged claw. Preserve the bent centreline and all attachment shell intervals; do not push outward beyond the frozen envelope or replace it with a straight rod.
2. Convert each plate from a tall isolated point into a low, broad pentagonal shingle: retain the measured buried base footprint and twelve-piece rhythm, but spread its visual base along the back and use a shallower sloped exposed crown. It must remain attached in side, rear, and top views and cannot become extra spikes, horns, or a new signal.
3. Fail before renders unless all modified limb caps still pass full-footprint torso intervals and claw overlap, all plates retain every measured base hit, all parts are closed/positive/two-face edged, bounds remain frozen, and parity is exact. Re-capture the eight neutral/mobile views.

This is a single focused silhouette-and-armour repair within the authorized lane, not an opening for a new species feature or a bake. R1 should not be serialized to the runtime while held.
